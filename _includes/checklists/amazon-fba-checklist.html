TaskDescriptionChecklist (Yes/No)
1. Product Research
Find Your NicheExplore different markets and find a profitable niche that excites you.
Spy on CompetitorsTake a close look at what your top competitors are doing. Learn from them!
Check Market DemandUse tools to see if there's enough demand for your product idea.
Confirm Profit MarginsMake sure you're making enough profit after Amazon fees and other costs.
Double-Check Product RestrictionsEnsure your product isn’t restricted or requires special approval on Amazon.
2. Supplier Sourcing
Find Reliable SuppliersBrowse platforms like Alibaba to find trustworthy suppliers.
Order SamplesAlways, always, always get samples before placing a big order.
Negotiate the Best DealTalk pricing, minimum orders, and payment terms with your supplier.
Agree on Shipping TermsDecide if you want to go with FOB or EXW for shipping.
Finalize the Purchase OrderPut everything in writing: product details, pricing, shipping, and payment terms.
3. Product Development
Design Your PackagingMake sure your packaging is attractive and Amazon-compliant.
Brand Your ProductEnsure your product has a clear brand identity with a unique identifier (UPC, EAN, GTIN).
Set Up Quality ControlEstablish a quality control process to catch any issues before they ship.
4. Amazon Account Setup
Create Your Seller AccountSet up your Amazon Seller account. Choose between Individual or Professional.
Link Your Bank AccountSet up your payment method to receive those payouts.
Submit Tax InfoFill in all necessary tax information to avoid any hiccups.
5. Product Listing Optimization
Create Your ListingsWrite an engaging title, bullet points, and description. Don’t forget to add high-res images.
Add KeywordsDo some keyword research and add them to your listing to help it get found.
Set Your PriceChoose a competitive price that aligns with your profit goals.
Consider VariationsIf your product has different sizes or colors, set up variations.
Register Your BrandIf you’ve got a trademark, enrol in Amazon Brand Registry for extra benefits.
6. Shipping to Amazon
Label Your ProductsPrint and attach FNSKU labels on each product.
Prep Your ProductsMake sure your products meet Amazon’s prep requirements (e.g., poly-bagging, bubble wrapping).
Create a Shipping PlanLog into Seller Central and create a shipping plan to send your goods to Amazon.
Arrange ShippingSet up your freight forwarder if you're importing goods.
Print Shipping LabelsGet those shipping labels printed and stuck on your boxes.
Send Products to AmazonShip your products off to Amazon FBA warehouses.
7. Launch Strategy
Get Those First ReviewsJoin Amazon’s Early Reviewer Program or ask initial customers for reviews.
Start Amazon PPC CampaignsSet up advertising campaigns to drive traffic to your listing.
Offer Launch PromotionsConsider discounts or coupons to get the ball rolling.
Promote on Social MediaSpread the word on your social media channels.
Partner with InfluencersCollaborate with influencers to reach a wider audience.
8. Inventory Management
Set Reorder PointsCalculate when you need to reorder to avoid running out of stock.
Monitor Inventory LevelsKeep an eye on your stock levels using Amazon’s tools.
Automate ReplenishmentConsider using inventory management software to streamline the process.
Watch Out for Storage FeesAvoid Amazon’s long-term storage fees by managing your inventory efficiently.
Perform Inventory SyncSync your inventory using SellerChamp’s tool across multiple channels, including Amazon. 
9. Customer Service
Respond to Inquiries QuicklyMake sure to reply to customer messages within 24 hours.
Handle Returns SmoothlyManage returns and refunds in line with Amazon’s policies.
Keep an Eye on FeedbackRegularly check and respond to product reviews and customer feedback.
10. Account Health Management
Track Performance MetricsKeep an eye on Order Defect Rate, Late Shipment Rate, and other key metrics.
Stay CompliantRegularly review Amazon’s policies to ensure you’re in compliance.
Monitor Account HealthCheck your Account Health Dashboard in Seller Central.
11. Optimization and Scaling
A/B Test ListingsTry out different titles, images, and prices to see what works best.
Expand Your Product LineOnce you’ve got one product down, start researching new products.
Go GlobalConsider selling in other Amazon marketplaces, like Europe or Canada.
Review Your Advertising ROIRegularly check how well your ads are performing and adjust as needed.
Cut Unnecessary CostsLook for ways to reduce costs without compromising quality.
12. Financial Management
Track Your ExpensesUse accounting software to keep tabs on every dollar.
Manage Cash FlowKeep enough cash on hand to fuel your growth.
Stay Sales Tax CompliantMake sure you're collecting and remitting sales tax where required.
Analyze ProfitabilityRegularly review your profit margins and adjust strategies as needed.
13. Legal Considerations
Register TrademarksProtect your brand by registering trademarks.
Get InsuranceObtain product liability insurance if needed.
Set Up Your Business StructureMake sure your business structure is optimized for taxes and liability.
Stay Up to Date with ComplianceKeep track of legal requirements for your product category.
14. Continuous Learning and Improvement
Stay InformedFollow Amazon FBA and SellerChamp blogs and forums to keep up with the latest trends.
Attend Webinars and CoursesInvest in ongoing education to improve your skills.
Network with Other SellersJoin seller communities to share tips and get support.
Attend relevant Trade Shows, Conferences and EventsStay engaged by attending major events like Prosper Show, ASD Market Week, IRCE (Internet Retailer Conference & Exhibition), Global Sources Summit, White Label World Expo, and eCommerce Expo.
