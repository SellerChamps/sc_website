We’re always happy to show our community the improvements and enhancements that we’re currently working on.</p>
<p>Here are some of the main features that were rolled out in the last few weeks:</p>
<h2>Ability to connect any sales channel after a successful sign-up</h2>
<p>Now, SellerChamp™ enables you to connect to any sales channel the minute you create your account.</p>
<h2>Ability to print Price Labels for your items.</h2>
<p>The most awaited feature is here – price labels for any and all products can now be printed with a single click.</p>
<h2>Ability to view parent SKUs when the product is bundled.</h2>
<p>You can easily view all related Parent Bundle SKUs on a modal popup, as shown above.</p>
<h2>Ability to add Default Handling time and Shipping template name to your Custom Feeds.</h2>
<p>You can now add a default Handling Time and Shipping Template Name for your custom feeds. Missing values will be automatically assigned by SellerChamp™.</p>
<h2>Ability to specify Default Section at the Marketplace and Manifest settings.</h2>
<p>You can easily set your default Shop section for all Etsy items that you plan to add to your inventory by setting it to the Marketplace or Manifest level.</p>
<h2>Ability to add Sale Price to your Woo-Commerce Items</h2>
<p>You can easily set a Sale Price for all your Woo-Commerce items by navigating to the respective product’s Edit modal.</p>
<p>Note: Sale Price should be lower than your Selling Price</p>
<h2>Ability to increase or decrease the Recurring reprice rule simply by typing `%` or `$`.</h2>
<p>Yes, you’ve heard it correctly! Now, you can set your recurrent reprice rule value by increment, decrement, or flat price. You may also define whether you want to go for a certain percentage value or a static constant value.</p>
<h2>Ability to set the MSRP of an item via CSV upload.</h2>
<p>Create a new column called ‘MSRP’ and add it, along with its related SKU, to the CSV file. Upload this to the Manifest or Inventory and SellerChamp™ will automatically update the respective product’s MSRP.</p>
<h2>Ability to remove multiple variants using the Remove option.</h2>
<p>A lot of our current users are unable to delete multiple variants by selecting them from a list. Now, however, simply select which variants you want to delete and click on the “Options” button, where the choice to remove all of them can be found.</p>
<h2>Ability to remove a Child SKU from a Bundle listing.</h2>
<p>Once you are in the “Items in Bundle” modal popup, you can simply click the “Delete” icon of the Child SKU that you want to remove from the Bundle listing</p>
<h2>Ability to acknowledge a Walmart Order.</h2>
<p>On the Orders page, click on the “Options” button of a Walmart order marked as “Awaiting Shipment.” Upon clicking on “Acknowledge,” the system will automatically send a confirmation to Walmart, labeling the order as “acknowledged” on SellerChamp™.</p>
<h2>Ability to set SKU prefixes to skip items for your custom feed.</h2>
<p>At the Custom Feed settings, you may specify a SKU prefix that you would like the corresponding SKUs to skip during the import process.</p>
<h2>Ability to set a default category at the Manifest settings level.</h2>
<p>Now, you can specify a default Item Category at the Manifest settings, so whenever you scan a new item in the respective manifest, the system will automatically populate the specified item category</p>
<h2>Ability to sort items by Sales Rank.</h2>
<p>You can sort your items using the Sales Rank field on the Inventory and Manifest pages.</p>
<h2>Ability to tag a Woo-Commerce Item.</h2>
<p>If you add a tag to a Woo-Commerce item, the system will automatically push the tag to the platform’s marketplace and populate it with the respective item.</p>
<h2>Ability to view Revision History of a deleted item.</h2>
<p>As shown above, you can now view the Revision History of a deleted item.</p>
<p>We hope that you like all the changes that were created especially for you! Please feel free to contact us if you have any questions.</p>
<p>Have a good day and stay safe!</p>
