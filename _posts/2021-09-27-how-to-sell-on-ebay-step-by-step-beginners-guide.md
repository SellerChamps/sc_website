## Step 1: Create Your eBay Seller Account

Sign up at eBay.com, choose an individual or business account, and set up your payout method. A business account unlocks higher selling limits and more seller tools from the start.

## Step 2: Research What to Sell

Check **Sold listings** on eBay for items similar to what you're considering — this tells you real demand and real selling prices, not just what's currently listed.

## Step 3: Source Your Inventory

Whether it's retail arbitrage, wholesale, thrifted goods or your own handmade products, make sure your sourcing cost leaves real margin after eBay's final value fee (typically around 13% for most categories) and shipping.

## Step 4: Write a Listing That Converts

A strong listing needs: a keyword-rich title, clear photos from multiple angles, accurate item specifics, and a description that answers likely buyer questions upfront (condition, dimensions, what's included).

## Step 5: Price Competitively

Price against actual sold comps, not just current listings — current listings include items that aren't selling. Factor in eBay fees and shipping cost before setting your number.

## Step 6: Choose Your Shipping Method

Decide between calculated shipping (buyer pays actual cost) or free shipping (built into your price) — free shipping listings tend to convert better and rank slightly higher in search.

## Step 7: Fulfill Orders Promptly

Ship within your stated handling time and upload tracking immediately — late shipping is one of the fastest ways to damage your seller metrics and search visibility.

## Step 8: Build Your Feedback and Scale

Early sales should focus on clean transactions and fast shipping to build feedback score, which directly affects buyer trust and your search ranking over time.

## Selling on More Than Just eBay

Once you've got eBay working, the same listing and sourcing process usually works on Amazon, Walmart and Etsy too. SellerChamp lets you list once and push the same inventory to every channel, with stock synced automatically so you never oversell.
