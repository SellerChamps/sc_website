## What the Amazon Affiliate Program Is

Amazon Associates (the Amazon Affiliate Program) pays a commission to website owners, bloggers and influencers who drive traffic to Amazon product pages that result in a sale. It's free to join and open to anyone with an active site, blog or social following.

## How It Works for Sellers, Not Just Affiliates

If you sell on Amazon, you can use the affiliate program from the other direction — recruiting content creators, bloggers and influencers in your niche to link to your own listings, giving you an additional traffic source beyond Amazon's own search and ads.

## Setting Up as an Amazon Seller to Benefit From Affiliates

1. Make sure your listing is affiliate-friendly: strong images, complete bullet points, and competitive reviews — affiliates won't promote a weak listing.
2. Reach out to niche bloggers, YouTubers or Instagram accounts relevant to your product category.
3. Consider Amazon's **Brand Referral Bonus** if you're brand-registered — it gives you a partial fee rebate on sales driven from outside traffic, including affiliate links.

## Why External Traffic Helps Your Amazon Ranking Too

Amazon's algorithm rewards listings that bring in external traffic converting into sales — it signals demand beyond Amazon's own search, which can help your organic ranking over time.

## Combining Affiliate Traffic with a Multi-Channel Strategy

Driving outside traffic to Amazon works even better when that same catalog is also listed and selling on eBay, Walmart and your own store — you're not putting all your growth in one channel's hands.
