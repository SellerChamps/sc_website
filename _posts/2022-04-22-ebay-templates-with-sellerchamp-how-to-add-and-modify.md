## Why eBay Listing Templates Matter

A consistent template across your listings builds buyer trust and saves significant time compared to formatting every listing from scratch — especially once you're managing dozens or hundreds of SKUs.

## Adding a New Template in SellerChamp

1. Go to **Templates** in your SellerChamp dashboard.
2. Select **Create New Template** and choose eBay as the target channel.
3. Build your layout — header, description structure, policies section, and image placement.
4. Save and name the template so you can reuse it across similar product types.

## Applying a Template to Your Listings

When creating or bulk-editing eBay listings, select your saved template from the dropdown — SellerChamp applies the formatting automatically while keeping your actual product details (title, price, specifics) unique to each listing.

## Modifying an Existing Template

Open the template from your **Templates** list, make your changes, and save. Note that edits apply going forward to new or re-synced listings — already-live listings using the old version stay as-is unless you push an update.

## Using Different Templates for Different Product Lines

Most sellers build 2-3 templates — one per product category or price tier — rather than one-size-fits-all, since a template that works for apparel often doesn't fit electronics or collectibles well.

## Templates Across More Than Just eBay

SellerChamp templates aren't limited to eBay — the same system lets you build consistent, channel-appropriate templates for Amazon, Walmart, Etsy and Shopify too, so your listing quality stays consistent everywhere you sell.
