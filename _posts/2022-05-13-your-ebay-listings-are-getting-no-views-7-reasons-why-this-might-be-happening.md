Zero views on a listing you spent real time on is frustrating — and usually fixable. Here are the 7 most common reasons it happens, and what to do about each one.

## 1. Your Title Is Missing the Keywords Buyers Actually Search

eBay's search algorithm (Cassini) matches listings to search terms mostly through the title. A clever or vague title that skips the brand, model, size or defining attribute simply never surfaces for the searches that would find it.

## 2. Your Price Is Off the Market

Priced noticeably above (or suspiciously below) what similar sold listings show, and eBay's search ranking — plus buyer trust — both work against you. Check **Sold listings** for your exact item before setting a price.

## 3. You're in the Wrong Category

A listing in the wrong or an overly broad category won't show up when buyers filter by category-specific attributes. Double-check against a similar, well-selling listing.

## 4. Your Photos Aren't Pulling Clicks

eBay search is a visual scroll — a blurry, dark, or single stock photo gets scrolled past even if it ranks. Listings with multiple clear photos get more clicks for the same position.

## 5. Your Listing Is Too New (Or Too Old) in the Algorithm's Eyes

Cassini favors listings with some sales history and engagement, but also demotes stale, never-selling items over time. A listing that's sat with zero activity for months can fall in rank even if nothing else changed.

## 6. Your Seller Metrics Are Dragging You Down

Late shipping, low feedback score, or a high defect rate all lower your account-wide search visibility — not just on the one problem listing.

## 7. You're Missing Item Specifics

Incomplete item specifics (brand, size, color, etc.) mean your listing doesn't show up when buyers filter search results by those attributes — even if your title is strong.

## The Fix That Covers Most of These at Once
