## What a High-Ranking Amazon Listing Actually Looks Like

Top-ranking listings share the same structural elements: a title that leads with brand and key attributes, 5-7 benefit-driven bullet points, high-resolution images (including lifestyle and infographic shots), and backend search terms that cover synonyms your title doesn't.

## Title Template

`[Brand] [Product Name] - [Key Feature 1], [Key Feature 2], [Size/Quantity/Color]`

Example: *"SellerChamp Pro Barcode Scanner - Wireless, 2.4GHz, Long Battery Life, Compatible with Amazon FBA"*

## Bullet Point Template

Each bullet should open with a benefit in caps, then explain it:

- **FAST SETUP** - Connect in under 2 minutes with no additional software required
- **BUILT TO LAST** - Reinforced housing rated for daily warehouse use
- **WORKS WITH YOUR WORKFLOW** - Compatible with [specific platforms/systems]

## Description Template

Open with the core problem the product solves, follow with 2-3 short paragraphs on how it solves it, and close with what's included in the box and your return/warranty policy.

## Image Checklist

1. Main image: pure white background, product fills 85% of frame
2. Infographic: key specs and dimensions overlaid on the product shot
3. Lifestyle: product in actual use
4. Comparison: your product vs. the alternative, if relevant
5. Packaging/unboxing shot for trust

## Backend Search Terms

Use this field for synonyms, misspellings and related terms you didn't fit in the title or bullets — this is indexed but never shown to buyers.

Applying this template to one listing is easy; keeping dozens or hundreds consistent across Amazon, eBay, Walmart and Shopify by hand is where most sellers fall behind. SellerChamp lets you build a listing template once and push it, formatted correctly, to every channel you sell on.
