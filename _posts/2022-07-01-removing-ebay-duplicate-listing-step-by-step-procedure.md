## Why Duplicate Listings Happen

Most duplicates aren't a seller mistake — they're a side effect of listing on multiple channels or re-listing an item manually instead of relisting the original. eBay's policy flags duplicate listings because they clutter search results and create a bad buyer experience, and repeated violations can limit your selling privileges.

## How to Spot a Duplicate Listing

Check **My eBay > Selling > Active** and sort by title. Two listings with the same (or near-identical) title, photos and description for the same item are duplicates. eBay's own listing-violation emails will also flag these directly if their system catches it first.

## Step-by-Step: Removing a Duplicate Listing

1. Go to **My eBay > Selling > Active**.
2. Identify which listing has existing views, watchers or bids — keep that one.
3. Select the duplicate and choose **End listing**.
4. Pick a reason ("Listing error" is usually accurate here).
5. Confirm. Don't recreate it immediately — wait until you've fixed the root cause below.

## Fixing the Root Cause

Duplicate listings are almost always a sync problem: the same SKU gets listed twice because inventory tools, spreadsheets or manual re-lists lose track of what's already live. SellerChamp lists each SKU to eBay once and keeps it synced across every other channel you sell on, so the duplicate-listing problem stops recurring instead of needing cleanup every few weeks.
