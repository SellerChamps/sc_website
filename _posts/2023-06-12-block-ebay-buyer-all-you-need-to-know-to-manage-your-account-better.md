## When Blocking a Buyer Makes Sense

Not every difficult buyer needs to be blocked — but repeated non-payment, abusive messages, return abuse, or bidding from a buyer in a region you don't ship to are all valid reasons to cut off future purchases from a specific account.

## How to Block a Buyer on eBay

1. Go to **Account Settings > Site Preferences**.
2. Under **Buyer management**, select **Block bidders/buyers**.
3. Add the buyer's username to the blocked list — you can add several at once.
4. Save. The blocked buyer can no longer bid on or buy from your listings.

## Setting Automatic Buyer Requirements

Beyond blocking individuals, eBay lets you set blanket **Buyer Requirements** — block buyers with unpaid item strikes, buyers in locations you don't ship to, or accounts with a low feedback score. This prevents problem buyers from reaching your listings in the first place, instead of reacting after the fact.

## What Blocking Doesn't Do

Blocking stops future purchases — it doesn't cancel an existing order or refund a return already in progress. Handle any open transaction through eBay's standard order and return tools first, then block the buyer separately.

## Keep Your Account Healthy Beyond Buyer Blocks

Buyer issues are only one side of account health — listing accuracy and on-time fulfillment matter just as much to your seller standing.
