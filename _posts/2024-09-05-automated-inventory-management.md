<p><b>Automated inventory management</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> is the backbone of successful businesses. How? </span></p><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Well, let&#8217;s say you own an e-commerce business. Life is good. Your customers love your products and you have more business than you’ve ever had.</span></p><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">And then your bestseller runs out of stock. Pretty soon, you find that a failure in your inventory has caused a chain reaction of failures you didn’t know existed. </span></p><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Inventory management can make or break a business, especially in e-commerce. Get it wrong and you will not only lose customers but also lose money by compromising efficiency, overstocking, and understocking. </span></p><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">An automated inventory management is one of the best ways to </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">streamline e-commerce operation</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">. </span></p><p><iframe title="YouTube video player" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/hVZgRbIl1cA?si=_akk31hJsWEOXMRq" width="560" height="315" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen="allowfullscreen"></iframe></p><h2>What is Automated Inventory Management?</h2><p><span style="font-weight: 400;"> An automated inventory management refers to the use of technology to track your inventory without you or your employees having to manually add data to a spreadsheet or update stock levels. The software does that for you.</span></p><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Now, despite the fact that this sounds like the easiest thing in the world, it really isn’t.</span></p><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">It simply represents one of the most efficient and fool-proof ways of ensuring you have enough (or less) products for customers without having to hire more employees, or ask your existing staff to track and reorder products after they are slow to sell each month. </span></p><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Basically, this is all about minimizing human error so that you can scale your </span><a href="https://sellerchamp.com/blog/ecommerce-branding-strategies/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">e-commerce branding strategy</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">.</span></p><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">However, after you </span><a href="https://youtu.be/KsJR4_TIjvc?si=2xnsMUnkzfDJHlM7"><span style="font-weight: 400;">sync inventory across multiple markets</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, why should you care about automating inventory management?</span><br /><iframe title="YouTube video player" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/KsJR4_TIjvc?si=VUlNQ3Z7gH44lD2Y" width="560" height="315" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen="allowfullscreen"></iframe></p>

</div>

<p><b><i>Here’s the scary truth: </i></b><a href="https://www.zippia.com/advice/supply-chain-statistics/"><b><i>43 per cent of small businesses</i></b></a><b><i> are unable to accurately measure their inventory, and 24 percent of companies say they have had to overstock simply to avoid the pain of product shortages.</i></b></p>
</div>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-9e8d3dc elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="9e8d3dc" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Moreover,</span><span style="font-weight: 400;"> approximately </span><a href="https://www.reliantfunding.com/why-properly-managing-your-inventory-takes-priority/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">34% of businesses</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> who use manually-recorded inventory numbers are subject to human error such as: </span></p><ul><li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Counting mistakes</span></li><li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Data entry errors</span></li><li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Forgotten notes </span></li></ul><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Here are 9 habits to master automated inventory management. </span></p><h3><span style="font-weight: 400;">1. Choose the Right Software Tool</span></h3><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">That might seem obvious, but not all inventory management software is built the same way. Some are designed for specific industries.</span></p><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Some can integrate with your existing tools. </span></p><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Do you run an ebay store? Look for something that can help in </span><a href="https://sellerchamp.com/blog/sourcing-inventory-for-ebay-store-top-15-places-to-get-stuff-to-sell/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">sourcing inventory for ebay listing</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">. </span></p><p><b>A user-friendly bonus tip:</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> pick software like </span><a href="https://sellerchamp.com/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">SellerChamp</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> that is scalable. </span></p><h3><span style="font-weight: 400;">2. Integrate Your Systems</span></h3><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Your inventory system needs to be able to communicate with your sales platform, your accounting software and your shipping solutions.</span></p><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">If you sell 50 items on eBay and your inventory system doesn’t update the number of items sold, by the time you log in and clear out those items, you’ve oversold and upset your customers.</span></p><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">You will be saved from inputting stock data every time you get an order because a high-functioning system will make sure that every sale updates your stock in real time, thus saving you from the risk of overselling and cutting down on human error.</span></p><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">In fact, many modern systems allow for one-click integrations, meaning procuring good add-ons to your system will make your life a lot easier and help </span><a href="https://sellerchamp.com/blog/how-to-attract-online-customers-13-tried-and-tested-tips/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">attract online customers</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">. </span></p><p>
<br /><iframe title="YouTube video player" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/1rbiFIKWtQA?si=sak0DoxCE1AHBEsU" width="560" height="315" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen="allowfullscreen"></iframe></p><h3><span style="font-weight: 400;">3. Use Real-Time Data</span></h3><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The most obvious benefit is of course that everything is automated, so you’re able to keep real-time track of your stock. Instead of someone going in to update stock levels, everything is monitored automatically.</span></p><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Think about it this way: real-time data lets you anticipate demand, tweak reorder points while orders are moving one at a time, and shift to adjust to sales trends as they play out in real time.</span></p><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">If one item is selling quickly, you’ll know right away so you can restock before it sells out. No more surprise stockouts!</span></p><h3><span style="font-weight: 400;">4. Leverage Demand Forecasting</span></h3><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Up your automation game! Consistent demand forecasting leverages historical trends to predict future sales.</span></p><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Think of it, the holiday season is almost upon us.</span></p><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">This year, rather than making an estimate of how much stock you’re going to need, you can examine what happened last year – you can look at the actual sales – and you can look at current market trends and put together a system to estimate how much stock you’re going to need.</span></p><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">It won’t overestimate, it won’t underestimate; it will forecast exactly what you need. </span></p><h3><span style="font-weight: 400;">5. Set Automated Reordering Rules</span></h3><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Do you ridiculously stack your underwear drawer because you can’t remember if you ordered more?</span></p><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Here’s an automated inventory management feature that lets you set up automated reorder rules.</span></p><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">For example, you can enter the reorder point (the minimum quantity level before the system should issue a reorder) and the system will place an order with the supplier when your stock gets too low.</span></p><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">It’s a bit like insurance for the items in your inventory: you’ll always know when to re-order, and you can change these levels if demand changes seasonally or as you anticipate promotions.</span></p><h3><span style="font-weight: 400;">6. Keep an Eye on Dead Stock</span></h3><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Dead stock pokes fun at you by not moving. Yes, it is one of the silent killers of the coveted profitability – capital is tied up in unwanted merchandise taking up storage.</span></p><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">A computerized inventory system will make it easy to recognize slow-movers. Run reports to see what isn’t selling through, and make intelligent decisions — discount, bundle or remove — so that money doesn’t sit idle on a shelf.</span></p><h3><span style="font-weight: 400;">7. Track Multiple Locations</span></h3><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">If you have multiple stores or warehouses, keeping on top of what stock you have can get a bit hectic.</span></p><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Decentralized inventory management becomes a nightmare. Automation centralizes your inventory management across all the locations you run.</span></p><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Instead of tending to each warehouse or store individually, you can have a single system that displays in real time what you’re carrying in every location. Transfers, redistribution and multi location reorders become a snap.</span></p><h3><span style="font-weight: 400;">8. Monitor Supplier Performance</span></h3><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Your business is only as good as the suppliers you’re working with. An automatic system allows you to track the performance metrics – lead time, fill rate, delivery accuracy – of your suppliers.</span></p><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Poor performance on the part of one of your suppliers means poor inventory levels for you – and poor customer service.</span></p><h3><span style="font-weight: 400;">9. Embrace Continuous Improvement</span></h3><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The thing is, automated inventory management isn’t a ‘set it and forget it’ system.</span></p><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">You should be constantly checking your performance metrics and tweaking when necessary.</span></p><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">An added bonus of automation is that success is truly achievable, because you can quickly see in quantified terms what is working or not, and make data-driven fine-tuning adjustments to your inventory.</span></p><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Keep an eye on how sales trends develop, and use the system to track dead stock, reordering points, and supplier performance, all regularly and progressively tweaking to refine your system.</span></p><h2>Tired of having to juggle between your spreadsheets and stockout alerts?</h2>
