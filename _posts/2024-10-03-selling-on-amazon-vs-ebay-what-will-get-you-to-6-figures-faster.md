However, these new businesses manage to build credibility with ease on Amazon. </span></li><li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">The</span><b> level of automation </b><span style="font-weight: 400;">available on Amazon’s FBA is </span><b>not possible on eBay</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">. </span></li></ol><h2><span style="font-size: 24pt;">Amazon vs. eBay: What Can Help You Hit The Magic Six-Figure Mark Faster?</span></h2><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Speaking generally, it is safe to say that Amazon has the edge when it comes to rapid business growth.
