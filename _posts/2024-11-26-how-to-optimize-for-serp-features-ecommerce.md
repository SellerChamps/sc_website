<h2 style="font-size: 24pt; font-weight: 500;">What Are SERP Features and Why They Matter </h2>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-719906a elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="719906a" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Search Engine Results Page (SERP) features are the extra elements Google displays beyond blue links. For ecommerce sellers, these include:</span>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Shopping and product carousels</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Featured snippets</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">People Also Ask boxes</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Review stars</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Image and video packs</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Knowledge panels</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Local packs</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Sitelinks</span></li>
</ul>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">These features grab attention quickly.</span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-868dd28 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="868dd28" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">

<p><b>Did You Know? </b><span style="font-weight: 400;">According to </span><a href="https://www.nimbleway.com/blog/serp-data-ecommerce-use-cases?"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Nimbleway,</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> the number one organic result earns </span><b>28 percent of clicks</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">, while </span><b>99.7 percent of users never move past page one</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">. </span></p>
</div>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">If SERP features dominate the page, not optimizing for them means losing traffic to competitors.</span>

<h2 style="font-size: 24pt; font-weight: 500;">Step 1: Check Which Features Already Appear for Your Keywords</h2>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-9b48349 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="9b48349" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Start by auditing where your store stands. </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Tools like SEMrush, Ahrefs, and Conductor allow you to filter by SERP features.</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> This shows whether your target keywords trigger shopping carousels, snippets, or PAA boxes. You can also track which features competitors dominate.</span>
</div>

<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-5d142fa elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="5d142fa" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">

<h2 style="font-size: 24pt; font-weight: 500;">Step 2: Use Schema Markup to Unlock Rich Results</h2>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-658676d elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="658676d" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Schema markup is how you communicate with Google in a structured way. It tells search engines what your products, reviews, and FAQs mean, not just what they say.</span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-f229d15 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="f229d15" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">For ecommerce sites, the most effective schema types include:</span>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Product schema</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> for details like price, brand, and availability</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Review schema</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> for star ratings and testimonials</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>FAQ schema</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> for buyer questions</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Breadcrumb schema</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> for navigation clarity</span></li>
</ul>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Conductor highlights schema as critical for winning result types like rich snippets and review stars. Make sure your </span><b>JSON-LD code is accurate</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">, consistent, and regularly updated. Google rewards fresh and trustworthy data.</span>
</div>
</div>

<h2 style="font-size: 24pt; font-weight: 500;">Step 3: Target Featured Snippets and People Also Ask </h2>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-32aa8af elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="32aa8af" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Featured snippets and People Also Ask boxes are gold for ecommerce. They push your content above organic results and establish your authority.</span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-6c4bf96 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="6c4bf96" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">To earn them:</span>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Format content with H2 and H3 headings for each question</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Provide short, direct answers in under 40 words</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Use bullet points and tables where possible</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Improve clarity and formatting compared to the current snippet</span></li>
</ul>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Search Engine Land notes that content structured for snippets often ends up pulled into AI overviews, too, giving you even more reach.</span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-7feb86a elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="7feb86a" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<p><b>Did You Know? </b><span style="font-weight: 400;">Product reviews are one of the most stable and influential features, visible in </span><b>over </b><a href="https://www.accuranker.com/blog/the-evolution-of-serp-features-key-changes-and-how-to-adapt"><b>80 percent of ecommerce SERPs</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">. </span></a></p>

<h2 style="font-size: 24pt; font-weight: 500;">Step 4: Win Shopping Carousels, Images, and Video Packs </h2>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-a998dc8 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="a998dc8" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Shopping features are where ecommerce sellers can beat even big marketplaces. To appear, your Google Merchant Center feed must be complete and correct.</span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-762dd51 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="762dd51" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Key actions include:</span>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Upload high-quality product images</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Keep stock availability current</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Ensure pricing and shipping details are accurate</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Write keyword-rich product titles and descriptions</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Collect and display reviews</span></li>
</ul>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Image packs require descriptive alt text and optimized file names. For video packs, create short product demos, unboxings, or tutorials. Add video schema and optimize titles and descriptions. Growth by Data shows these optimizations give smaller retailers a chance to compete with giants.</span>
</div>

<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-0627484 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="0627484" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">

</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-5b0079c elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="5b0079c" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
