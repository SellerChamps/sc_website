<h2 style="font-size: 24pt; font-weight: 500;">Mercari vs eBay: What’s Better?</h2>

<h3 class="elementor-heading-title elementor-size-default">1. Platform Overview: Simplicity vs Reach </h3>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-56db0cd elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="56db0cd" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Mercari</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> is a mobile-first resale marketplace that prioritizes ease of use. It’s fast-growing in the U.S., with a user base that skews younger and more lifestyle-focused.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>eBay</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> is a global e-commerce beast. It’s ideal for collectibles, electronics, rare finds, and bulk inventory—offering more flexibility but with a steeper learning curve. </span></li>
</ul>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-1016f56 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="1016f56" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">eBay boasts over </span><a href="https://www.chargeflow.io/blog/ebay-statistics?"><span style="font-weight: 400;">135 million global buyers.</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Mercari’s reach is smaller, with</span><a href="https://thesmallbusinessblog.com/mercari-users/">
<b>20 million monthly active users,</b></a>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">but laser-focused on casual U.S. shoppers looking for quick, affordable deals. </span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-429c8a8 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="429c8a8" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">If you’re selling second-hand fashion or household goods to a younger crowd, Mercari might feel like home. If you’ve got vintage games, car parts, or international demand? eBay’s your empire. </span>
</div>
</div>

<h3 class="elementor-heading-title elementor-size-default">2. Fees: Who Eats More of Your Profits? </h3>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-064610c elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="064610c" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Understanding fees is critical for profitability.</span>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Mercari</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: Flat 10% selling fee + 2.9% payment processing + $0.50 transaction fee.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>eBay</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: Final value fees between 10–15% (category-dependent), $0.30 per order, and optional paid subscriptions for higher-volume sellers.</span></li>
</ul>
<b>Example</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: Sell a $40 toy.</span>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Mercari fees = $4 + ~$1.66 = $5.66</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">eBay fees = ~$5.50–$6 (plus listing fee if you exceed the 250/month free limit)</span></li>
</ul>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">For lower-priced goods under $50, </span><b>Mercari usually saves you more</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">. For higher-value or bulk items, </span><b>eBay can be more scalable</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">.</span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-51a7c0f elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="51a7c0f" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Pro tip: eBay’s fee structures get more complex but offer better ROI if you know how to work the system (store plans, category pricing, etc.).</span>
</div>

<h3 class="elementor-heading-title elementor-size-default">3. Listing Process: Speed vs Control </h3>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-79fc659 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="79fc659" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Mercari</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> makes it fast: 12 photos max, mobile-first form, basic item descriptions.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>eBay</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> gives you advanced options: auctions, Buy It Now, Best Offer, 24 photos, custom fields, product identifiers.</span></li>
</ul>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">If you want lightning-fast listing, </span><b>Mercari feels like Instagram with a price tag</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">. If you&#8217;re managing a serious storefront, </span><b>eBay gives you the control pros crave</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">.</span>
</div>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Also: eBay lets you batch list, schedule listings, and customize store branding—features Mercari lacks.</span>

<h3 class="elementor-heading-title elementor-size-default">4. Shipping: Streamlined vs Strategic </h3>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-a0e279d elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="a0e279d" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Mercari</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: Offers prepaid shipping via USPS, UPS, FedEx. Just print and ship. Optional local delivery through Uber for bulky items. The buyer can pay for shipping or seller.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>eBay</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: Full flexibility. Choose carriers, set prices, offer international options, add handling time.</span></li>
</ul>
<b>Mercari is perfect for beginners</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> or sellers who want plug-and-play logistics. </span><b>eBay is better for advanced sellers</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> who optimize shipping zones, international rates, and cost-saving hacks.</span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-8777887 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="8777887" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">One major bonus: eBay sellers can access deeper USPS/UPS discounts and global shipping program tools.</span>

<h3 class="elementor-heading-title elementor-size-default">5. Payment &amp; Seller Protections </h3>

<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-fa27526 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="fa27526" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Mercari</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: Payment is released once the buyer rates the seller (or after 3 days if they don’t). Disputes can be challenging.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>eBay</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: Faster payouts with managed payments. Better buyer/seller protection and dispute resolution. Clearer policies around returns.</span></li>
</ul>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">While both platforms protect buyers well, </span><b>eBay offers more structured seller support</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">—especially important if you scale operations.</span>

<h3 class="elementor-heading-title elementor-size-default">6. Audience: Who’s Shopping Where? </h3>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-925cf6a elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="925cf6a" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Mercari</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: Shoppers looking for deals on clothes, gadgets, games, and home items. Younger crowd, mobile-first.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>eBay</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: A mix of bargain hunters, collectors, and niche product seekers. International users are a huge part of the ecosystem.</span></li>
</ul>
<b>Mercari = lifestyle marketplace.</b>
<b>eBay = everything marketplace.</b>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-89c985f elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="89c985f" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<i><span style="font-weight: 400;">eBay hosts </span></i><a href="https://www.chargeflow.io/blog/ebay-statistics"><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">over 2.3 billion listings across 190+ countries,</span></i></a><i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> making it the go-to global marketplace for nearly every product niche. </span></i>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-7a26bfb elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="7a26bfb" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Have something rare, refurbished, or collectible? eBay will outperform. Selling gently used clothes or baby items? Mercari might sell faster.</span>
