<h2>1. Keyword Research for Product SEO
</h2>
<b>Search Engine Optimization (SEO)</b> is critical for ensuring that your website and product listings appear when potential customers search online. SEO not only makes your listings <b>searchable </b> but also ensures that they appear first in the sea of search results. Without good<a href="/blog/walmart-seo-mastering-keyword-optimization-for-top-rankings/"> SEO</a>, even the best products can get buried. <b>What the service includes:</b> Researching high-converting keywords, optimizing product meta tags, and creating SEO-friendly titles and descriptions. <b>Where to get it:</b>

<ul>
<li><b>Ahrefs</b> or <b>Ubersuggest</b>: Use these tools to simplify work and find high-volume keywords.</li>
<li><b>Freelance SEO experts</b> on Upwork, Fiverr, or other talent sites.</li>
</ul>

<h2>3. Photo Editing, Cropping & Resizing
</h2>
<a href="https://www.shopify.com/blog/ecommerce-photography">Professional photography</a> plays a major role in building trust and increasing conversions for an e-commerce store. For good imagery, take <b> high-quality pictures</b> with consistent branding/editing to convey professionalism. For <a href="https://www.shopify.com/blog/image-sizes">Shopify</a>, it is best to keep image sizes minimal, with the perfect width being <b>2500 pixels.</b> However, if you prefer larger images for your listings, Shopify allows a maximum of 5000 x 5000 pixels. <b>What the service includes:</b> Background editing/removal, standardizing image sizes, improving lighting, and optimizing for speed. <b>Where to get it:</b>

<ul><li><b>Pixelz</b> and <b>Pixlr</b>: Offer bulk e-commerce photo editing for quick processing.</li><li><b>Fiverr</b>: Great for budget-friendly options, especially for smaller businesses looking to outsource editing.</li><li><b>Canva Pro</b>: Do-it-yourself resizing for small teams.</li>
</ul>
