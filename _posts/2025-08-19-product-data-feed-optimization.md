<h3>The 5 Things Every High-Performing Feed Has
</h3>

<p>If you’re wondering where to start, these are the essentials every winning feed has in common.
</p>

<h3>1. Titles That Make People Click
</h3>

<p>Think of your title as a mini ad. The best ones are clear, specific, and stuffed (naturally) with the right keywords.
</p>

<p>Example: <i>Nike Air Zoom Pegasus 39 Men’s Running Shoes Size 10 Blue</i>.
</p>

<p>It’s not “Men’s Shoes” or “Nike Sneakers.” It’s specific, searchable, and clickable.
</p>

<h3>2. Descriptions That Sell Benefits, Not Just Features
</h3>

<p>Specs are boring. Sell the <i>why</i>, not just the <i>what</i>.
</p>

<p>Instead of: “Polyester backpack with 5 compartments”
</p>

<p>Say: <i>Stay organized on the go with a lightweight, water-resistant backpack that keeps your gear safe and your shoulders comfortable.</i>
</p>

<h3>3. Images That Make People Want to Touch the Screen
</h3>

<p>Your photos are your silent salespeople. Make them work for you. Use high-res images, clean backgrounds, multiple angles, and lifestyle shots that show your product in action.
</p>

<h3>4. Data That Algorithms Love
</h3>

<p>Platforms like Google, Meta, and TikTok read your feed like a book. If you give them complete, consistent info—GTIN, MPN, price, category, size—they’ll push your product higher in search results.
</p>

<h4>5. Constant Updates
</h4>

<p>Your prices change. Stock changes. Trends change. If your feed doesn’t keep up, you’ll slide down the rankings fast. Update at least once a week.
</p>
