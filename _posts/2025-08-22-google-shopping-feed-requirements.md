<h2>Image & Feed Specifications
</h2>

<p>Google is picky about images—and for good reason. Shoppers decide in seconds based on visuals.
</p>

<ul>
<li><b>Minimum image size:</b> 100×100 px (non-apparel), 250×250 px (apparel).

<p></li>
<li><b>No watermarks, logos, or promotional text.</b><b>

<p></b></li>
<li><b>Preferred formats:</b> JPEG, PNG, GIF.</li>
</ul>

<p>On the feed side:
</p>

<ul>
<li><b>Accepted formats:</b> .XML, .TXT, .TSV.

<p></li>
<li><b>Max feed size:</b> 4GB uncompressed.

<p></li>
<li><b>Language:</b> Attributes must be in English (even for international targeting).</li>
</ul>

<h2>Submission & Updates
</h2>

<p>Google requires you to keep your feed fresh:
</p>

<ul>
<li><b>Update at least every 30 days</b> (but ideally daily if you change prices or stock often).

<p></li>
<li><b>Submission methods:</b> Direct upload, scheduled fetch, Content API, or Google Sheets.

<p></li>
<li><b>Recent change (July 2025):</b> U.S. sellers<a href="https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/7052112"> no longer need to submit separate sales tax</a> attributes.</li>
</ul>

<p>Keeping your feed updated isn’t just about compliance. A <a href="https://www.wakeupdata.com/">study by WakeupData </a>shows that <b>up-to-date pricing improves click-through rates by 20%</b>.
</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
</h2>

<h3>1. How does identifier_exists work when a product has no GTIN?
</h3>

<p><b>A: </b> If a product doesn’t have a GTIN, you can set identifier_exists to <i>FALSE.</i> Use this option cautiously, because products without unique identifiers may get less visibility in Google Shopping.
</p>

<h3>2. What are the best practices for naming attributes like shipping and availability?
</h3>

<p><b>A: </b> Always follow Google’s accepted values. For availability, <b>use in_stock, out_of_stock, or preorder only.</b> For shipping, provide the correct format: country, service, and price. Sticking to these rules prevents feed disapprovals.
</p>

<h3>3. Can I include multiple images per product?
</h3>

<p><b>A: </b><i>Yes, </i>you can add extra product photos using the additional_image_link attribute. Include lifestyle photos, close-ups, or different angles to build trust and increase click-through rates.
</p>

<h3>4. What happens if I submit outdated pricing?
</h3>

<p><b>A: </b> Google may <b> disapprove your products, or worse, suspend your account </b> if mismatches persist between your site and your feed.
</p>

<h3>5. How can I preview or test my feed before submission?
</h3>

<p><b>A: </b> You can use the<i> Merchant Center’s “Diagnostics” tab</i> to test feeds for errors before pushing them live.
</p>

<h3>6. When should I use supplemental feeds?
</h3>

<p><b>A: </b> Use them when you need to <b>add missing fields (like GTINs)</b> or optimize data without changing your main e‑commerce system.
</p>
