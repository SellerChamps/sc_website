<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">If you want your products to shine in Google Shopping ads or free listings, you need to get one thing right: </span><b>Google Shopping Feed Requirements</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">. </span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">These are the rules and data standards set by Google to ensure your product feed is complete, accurate, and ready for shoppers. Get them wrong, and your products could be disapproved. Get them right, and you can boost clicks, sales, and visibility.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Let’s break it down step by step so it’s easy to follow.</span></p>
<h2><span style="font-weight: 400;">Why Google Shopping Feed Requirements Matter</span></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Google Shopping is massive.</span><a href="https://www.klientboost.com/google/google-shopping-statistics/">
<b>85% of all product searches</b></a><b> start on Google or Amazon</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">. That means your product feed is the backbone of your visibility. If you miss required attributes or upload poor data, you’ll either:</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Get disapproved listings.</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
<p></span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Waste ad spend.</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
<p></span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Or simply lose out on sales to competitors with better feeds.</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Think of your feed as your store’s digital shelf. The cleaner, more detailed, and optimized it is, the more shoppers will pick your product over someone else’s.</span></p>
<p><b>Check out: </b><a href="https://sellerchamp.com/blog/online-marketplaces-in-usa-for-ecommerce-sellers/"><b>Top 10 Online Marketplaces in USA for E-Commerce Sellers</b></a></p>

<h2><span style="font-weight: 400;">Optional (But Powerful) Attributes</span></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">These aren’t always mandatory, but can dramatically improve your visibility and clicks:</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Color, size, gender, age_group</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> (vital for apparel).</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
<p></span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Material &amp; pattern</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> (helps niche searches).</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
<p></span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Google_product_category</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> (improves targeting).</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
<p></span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Product_type</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> (your category structure).</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Adding these isn’t just about compliance—it’s about getting found by the right shopper. For example, “Women’s Blue Running Shoes Size 8” is far more likely to convert than just “Running Shoes.”</span></p>
<h2><span style="font-weight: 400;">Image &amp; Feed Specifications</span></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Google is picky about images—and for good reason. Shoppers decide in seconds based on visuals.</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Minimum image size:</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> 100×100 px (non-apparel), 250×250 px (apparel).</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
<p></span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>No watermarks, logos, or promotional text.</b><b>
<p></b></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Preferred formats:</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> JPEG, PNG, GIF.</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">On the feed side:</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Accepted formats:</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> .XML, .TXT, .TSV.</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
<p></span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Max feed size:</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> 4GB uncompressed.</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
<p></span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Language:</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Attributes must be in English (even for international targeting).</span></li>
</ul>

<h2><span style="font-weight: 400;">Submission &amp; Updates</span></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Google requires you to keep your feed fresh:</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Update at least every 30 days</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> (but ideally daily if you change prices or stock often).</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
<p></span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Submission methods:</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Direct upload, scheduled fetch, Content API, or Google Sheets.</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
<p></span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Recent change (July 2025):</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> U.S. sellers</span><a href="https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/7052112"><span style="font-weight: 400;"> no longer need to submit separate sales tax</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> attributes.</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Keeping your feed updated isn’t just about compliance. A </span><a href="https://www.wakeupdata.com/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">study by WakeupData </span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">shows that </span><b>up-to-date pricing improves click-through rates by 20%</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">.</span></p>
<h2><span style="font-weight: 400;">Advanced Feed Management &amp; Optimization</span></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Once you’ve nailed the basics, it’s time to optimize:</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Use rules and supplemental feeds</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> to enrich your main feed (e.g., add missing GTINs).</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
<p></span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Optimize product titles.</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Example: “Samsung Galaxy S24 – 128GB – Phantom Black” performs better than just “Samsung Galaxy Phone.”</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
<p></span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Troubleshoot errors quickly.</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Common issues include mismatched pricing, missing identifiers, or broken image links.</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Pro tip: Products with complete and optimized attributes have a</span><a href="https://www.datafeedwatch.com/blog/everything-you-need-to-know-about-google-shopping-feed-types">
<b>40% higher chance</b></a><b> of appearing in top Shopping results</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">
</span></p>
<h2><span style="font-weight: 400;">Different Feed Types</span></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Not all feeds are created equal. Here are the main types you might need:</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Primary feed</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: The main source of truth for your products.</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
<p></span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Supplemental feeds</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: Add or update data in your main feed.</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
<p></span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Local inventory feeds</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: Show nearby store availability.</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
<p></span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Promotions feeds</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: Highlight discounts and special offers.</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
<p></span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Product ratings feeds</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: Boost trust with reviews and star ratings.</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Using the right combination helps you win more eyeballs in Shopping results.</span></p>

<p></span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Use </span><b>high-quality images</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> with clear backgrounds.</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
<p></span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Optimize product titles and descriptions with natural keywords.</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">

<p></span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Monitor disapprovals and fix issues fast.</span></li>

<h2><span style="font-weight: 400;">Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)</span></h2>
<h3><span style="font-weight: 400;">1. How does identifier_exists work when a product has no GTIN?</span></h3>
<p><b>A: </b><span style="font-weight: 400;">If a product doesn’t have a GTIN, you can set identifier_exists to </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">FALSE.</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Use this option cautiously, because products without unique identifiers may get less visibility in Google Shopping.</span></p>
<h3><span style="font-weight: 400;">2. What are the best practices for naming attributes like shipping and availability?</span></h3>
<p><b>A: </b><span style="font-weight: 400;">Always follow Google’s accepted values. For availability, </span><b>use in_stock, out_of_stock, or preorder only.</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> For shipping, provide the correct format: country, service, and price. Sticking to these rules prevents feed disapprovals.</span></p>
<h3><span style="font-weight: 400;">3. Can I include multiple images per product?</span></h3>
<p><b>A: </b><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Yes, </span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">you can add extra product photos using the additional_image_link attribute. Include lifestyle photos, close-ups, or different angles to build trust and increase click-through rates.</span></p>
<h3><span style="font-weight: 400;">4. What happens if I submit outdated pricing?</span></h3>
<p><b>A: </b><span style="font-weight: 400;">Google may</span><b> disapprove your products, or worse, suspend your account </b><span style="font-weight: 400;">if mismatches persist between your site and your feed.</span></p>
<h3><span style="font-weight: 400;">5. How can I preview or test my feed before submission?</span></h3>
<p><b>A: </b><span style="font-weight: 400;">You can use the</span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Merchant Center’s “Diagnostics” tab</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> to test feeds for errors before pushing them live.</span></p>
<h3><span style="font-weight: 400;">6. When should I use supplemental feeds?</span></h3>
<p><b>A: </b><span style="font-weight: 400;">Use them when you need to </span><b>add missing fields (like GTINs)</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> or optimize data without changing your main e‑commerce system.</span></p>
