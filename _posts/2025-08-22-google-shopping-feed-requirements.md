<p></span></li>

<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Google requires you to keep your feed fresh:</span></p>

<h2><span style="font-weight: 400;">Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)</span></h2>
<h3><span style="font-weight: 400;">1. How does identifier_exists work when a product has no GTIN?</span></h3>
<p><b>A: </b><span style="font-weight: 400;">If a product doesn’t have a GTIN, you can set identifier_exists to </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">FALSE.</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Use this option cautiously, because products without unique identifiers may get less visibility in Google Shopping.</span></p>
<h3><span style="font-weight: 400;">2. What are the best practices for naming attributes like shipping and availability?</span></h3>
<p><b>A: </b><span style="font-weight: 400;">Always follow Google’s accepted values. For availability, </span><b>use in_stock, out_of_stock, or preorder only.</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> For shipping, provide the correct format: country, service, and price. Sticking to these rules prevents feed disapprovals.</span></p>
<h3><span style="font-weight: 400;">3. Can I include multiple images per product?</span></h3>
<p><b>A: </b><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Yes, </span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">you can add extra product photos using the additional_image_link attribute. Include lifestyle photos, close-ups, or different angles to build trust and increase click-through rates.</span></p>
<h3><span style="font-weight: 400;">4. What happens if I submit outdated pricing?</span></h3>
<p><b>A: </b><span style="font-weight: 400;">Google may</span><b> disapprove your products, or worse, suspend your account </b><span style="font-weight: 400;">if mismatches persist between your site and your feed.</span></p>
<h3><span style="font-weight: 400;">5. How can I preview or test my feed before submission?</span></h3>
<p><b>A: </b><span style="font-weight: 400;">You can use the</span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Merchant Center’s “Diagnostics” tab</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> to test feeds for errors before pushing them live.</span></p>
<h3><span style="font-weight: 400;">6. When should I use supplemental feeds?</span></h3>
<p><b>A: </b><span style="font-weight: 400;">Use them when you need to </span><b>add missing fields (like GTINs)</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> or optimize data without changing your main e‑commerce system.</span></p>
