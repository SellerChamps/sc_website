<h2>How Discounted Online Shipping Labels Actually Work
</h2>

<p>To understand what you’re getting, here’s the model:</p>

<ol>
<li>Shipping platforms or aggregators negotiate <b>volume-based discounted rates</b> with carriers. (Carriers offer better pricing for bulk shipping) </li>
<li>Those platforms (or software partners) pool demand across many sellers.</li>
<li>They pass those savings to you by letting you buy labels at those reduced rates.</li>
<li>You still get full label functionality — barcode, tracking, address, and payment.</li>
</ol>

<p>In other words, when you create an <b>online shipping label</b> via a system like SellerChamp, you’re tapping into those negotiated discounts — instead of paying the “retail” rate from the carrier’s website.</p>

<h2>Tips to Maximize Every Online Shipping Label
</h2>

<p>You want not just discounted labels, but <i>optimal</i> discounted labels:</p>

<h3>1. Use automation & rules
</h3>

<p>Let SellerChamp pick the cheapest acceptable carrier for you. Don’t choose manually every time.</p>

<h3>2. Watch dimensional weight (DIM)
</h3>

<p>Use the smallest box that fits. If you leave a lot of empty space, carriers will charge based on box size, not just weight.</p>

<h3>3. Batch label printing
</h3>

<p>Printing individually is slow. Do 20, 50, 100 labels in one go.</p>

<h3>4. Link your own carrier accounts
</h3>

<p>If you have UPS or FedEx negotiated accounts, link them. SellerChamp will compare those vs its discounted rates and pick the lower.</p>

<h3>5. Audit your label costs monthly
</h3>

<p>Export your labels, compare your average cost per label against past months, and spot anomalies.</p>

<h3>6. Be aware of exceptions
</h3>

<p>Some services (express, freight, special contracts) may not give you deep discounts. Always compare.</p>

<h3>7. Offer free shipping thresholds
</h3>

<p>Because your label costs are lower, you can absorb them better when you give “free shipping” above a certain cart value.</p>
