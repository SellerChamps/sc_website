<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Those platforms (or software partners) pool demand across many sellers.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">They pass those savings to you by letting you buy labels at those reduced rates.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">You still get full label functionality — barcode, tracking, address, and payment.</span></li>
