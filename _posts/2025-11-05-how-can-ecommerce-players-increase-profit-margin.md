<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<h2 style="font-size: 24pt; font-weight: 500;">What Is Contribution Margin and Why Does It Matter</h2>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-719906a elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="719906a" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Contribution margin is the amount left after you subtract all variable costs (like product cost, shipping, packaging, and ads) from your revenue.</span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-ca6721c elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="ca6721c" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<b>Formula:</b><b>
</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">Contribution Margin = Revenue – Variable Costs</span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-71198da elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="71198da" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Example:</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">If you sell a product for $50 and spend $35 on variable costs, your contribution margin is $15 (or 30%).</span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-90b939f elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="90b939f" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">The higher your contribution margin, the more profit you can use to cover fixed costs and grow faster.</span>

<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>High ad costs:</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Paid ads eat into profits when ROAS drops.</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
</span></li>
