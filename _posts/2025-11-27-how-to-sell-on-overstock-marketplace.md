<h2 style="font-size: 24pt; font-weight: 500;">How to Make Listings That Sell and Get Noticed</h2>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-32353ed elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="32353ed" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Selling on Overstock is not just about adding products; it’s about creating listings that attract buyers and making sure people see them. Follow these steps to get better results:</span>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Write Clear Titles and Descriptions</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: Include brand, product type, color, size, and key features. Simple, detailed descriptions help buyers understand your product and reduce confusion.</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Add High-Quality Images</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: Upload 4–6 photos. Show the product from different angles, include close-ups, and add lifestyle shots so buyers can imagine using it.</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Organize Variations</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: If your product has different sizes, colors, or styles, keep them organized. Tools like SellerChamp make managing multiple options easy.</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Set Smart Prices</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: Look at similar items and price your product fairly. Use dynamic or MSRP-based pricing for overstock or extra stock.</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Highlight Key Listings</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: Use bold or featured listing options to make your most important products stand out.</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Offer Bundles or Seasonal Deals</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: Combine related items or create seasonal packages to make listings more appealing and encourage buyers to purchase more.</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Promote Your Listings</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: Share your products on social media, email, or your website to reach more potential buyers.</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Track Performance</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: Keep an eye on views, orders, and sales. </span><a href="https://sellerchamp.com/features/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">SellerChamp</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> helps you track results and adjust listings to improve performance.</span></li>
</ul>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">By following these steps, your products will look professional, reach more buyers, and sell faster, turning extra stock into real profit.</span>

<h2 style="font-size: 24pt; font-weight: 500;">Tips for Apparel Sellers and Small Businesses </h2>
</div>

<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-61a5e99 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="61a5e99" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Selling clothes or running a small business on Overstock has its own challenges. Here are practical ways to make it easier and more effective:</span>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Provide Clear Sizes and Details</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: Include accurate size charts, fabric information, and care instructions. This helps buyers choose the right product and reduces returns.</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Use High-Quality Photos</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: Add lifestyle images and flat-lay shots so buyers can see the product clearly from different angles.</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Start Small</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: Use Overstock’s Main Street Revolution or similar options to test new products without large upfront risk.</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">
