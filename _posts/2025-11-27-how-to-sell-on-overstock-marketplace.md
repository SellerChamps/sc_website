<h2>Getting Started: Becoming an Overstock Seller
</h2>

<p>Before you start selling, it helps to understand the basic steps to set up your account and prepare your inventory.</p>

<h3>1. Apply and Get Approved
</h3>

<p>Sign up on Overstock’s Supplier Oasis portal. Submit your business details and at least three SKUs. Approval ensures your account is ready to sell.</p>

<h3>2. Understand Fees
</h3>

<ul><li>Listing fees: $0.10–$3.50 per item<br /></li><li>Final value fee: ~3% for items under $25<br /></li><li>Optional paid features: bold or featured listings</li><li>Subscription plan: ~$6.95/month for high-volume sellers</li>
</ul>

<h2>How to Make Listings That Sell and Get Noticed
</h2>

<p>Selling on Overstock is not just about adding products; it’s about creating listings that attract buyers and making sure people see them. Follow these steps to get better results:</p>

<ul>
<li><b>Write Clear Titles and Descriptions</b>: Include brand, product type, color, size, and key features. Simple, detailed descriptions help buyers understand your product and reduce confusion. </li>
<li><b>Add High-Quality Images</b>: Upload 4–6 photos. Show the product from different angles, include close-ups, and add lifestyle shots so buyers can imagine using it. </li>
<li><b>Organize Variations</b>: If your product has different sizes, colors, or styles, keep them organized. Tools like SellerChamp make managing multiple options easy. </li>
<li><b>Set Smart Prices</b>: Look at similar items and price your product fairly. Use dynamic or MSRP-based pricing for overstock or extra stock. </li>
<li><b>Highlight Key Listings</b>: Use bold or featured listing options to make your most important products stand out. </li>
<li><b>Offer Bundles or Seasonal Deals</b>: Combine related items or create seasonal packages to make listings more appealing and encourage buyers to purchase more. </li>
<li><b>Promote Your Listings</b>: Share your products on social media, email, or your website to reach more potential buyers. </li>
<li><b>Track Performance</b>: Keep an eye on views, orders, and sales. <a href="/features/">SellerChamp</a> helps you track results and adjust listings to improve performance.</li>
</ul>

<p>By following these steps, your products will look professional, reach more buyers, and sell faster, turning extra stock into real profit.</p>

<h2>Tips for Apparel Sellers and Small Businesses
</h2>

<p>Selling clothes or running a small business on Overstock has its own challenges. Here are practical ways to make it easier and more effective:</p>

<ul>
<li><b>Provide Clear Sizes and Details</b>: Include accurate size charts, fabric information, and care instructions. This helps buyers choose the right product and reduces returns. </li>
<li><b>Use High-Quality Photos</b>: Add lifestyle images and flat-lay shots so buyers can see the product clearly from different angles. </li>
<li><b>Start Small</b>: Use Overstock’s Main Street Revolution or similar options to test new products without large upfront risk. </li>
<li><b>Bundle Items</b>: Group related products together to move extra stock faster and make deals more appealing. </li>
<li><b>Follow Seasonal Trends</b>: Offer products in line with seasonal demand, like summer clothes or holiday bundles, to attract more buyers.</li>
</ul>
