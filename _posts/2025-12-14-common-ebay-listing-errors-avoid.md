<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<h2 style="font-size: 24pt; font-weight: 500;">1. Missing or Incorrect Item Specifics </h2>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-719906a elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="719906a" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Item specifics help eBay figure out exactly what your product is. When these fields are incomplete, outdated, or incorrect, eBay cannot match your listing to relevant searches. This is the number one eBay listing error for new and experienced sellers.</span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-71eda2b elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="71eda2b" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Many categories now require fields such as Brand,</span><a href="https://www.zikanalytics.com/blog/ebay-mpn/"><span style="font-weight: 400;"> MPN</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, Color, Size, Material, and UPC. Skipping them limits visibility instantly. Even optional specifics matter because they improve ranking and filter placement.</span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-855f0ee elementor-widget elementor-widget-heading" data-id="855f0ee" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="heading.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<h3 class="elementor-heading-title elementor-size-default">How to fix it</h3>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-394f48d elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="394f48d" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<ul><li><span style="font-weight: 400;">Complete every required and recommended field</span></li><li><span style="font-weight: 400;">Add UPC or MPN or select Does Not Apply</span></li><li><span style="font-weight: 400;">Encourage consistency by using product listing templates</span></li><li><span style="font-weight: 400;">Compare your specifics with the top sellers in your niche</span></li></ul>
</div>
</div>
</div>

<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<h2 style="font-size: 24pt; font-weight: 500;">2. Placing the Item in the Wrong Category</h2>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-9b48349 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="9b48349" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">A wrong category sends a confusing signal to eBay. When the category does not match the product, eBay treats the listing as irrelevant. This leads to low impressions or immediate rejection.</span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-898f5f7 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="898f5f7" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">This error often happens when sellers list quickly or bulk upload inventory without reviewing category changes.</span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-e5335c6 elementor-widget elementor-widget-heading" data-id="e5335c6" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="heading.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<h3 class="elementor-heading-title elementor-size-default">How to fix it</h3>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-a30e5ee elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="a30e5ee" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<ul><li><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Use eBay’s suggested categories</span></li><li><span style="font-weight: 400;">Double-check each bulk upload batch</span></li><li><span style="font-weight: 400;">Look at competitor listings for category inspiration</span></li></ul>
</div>
</div>
</div>

<h2 style="font-size: 24pt; font-weight: 500;">3. Variation Errors That Break the Listing</h2>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-2e0cb41 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="2e0cb41" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Variations help buyers choose between colors, sizes, or styles in one place. But they must follow eBay’s rules. Many sellers create variation groups that do not make sense together. Others add attributes that eBay does not support for that category.</span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-89d3a86 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="89d3a86" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">These conflicts lead to a blocked listing or missing variation fields.</span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-43411bc elementor-widget elementor-widget-heading" data-id="43411bc" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="heading.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<h3 class="elementor-heading-title elementor-size-default">How to fix it</h3>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-8194849 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="8194849" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<ul><li><span style="font-weight: 400;">Group only true variations of the same product</span></li><li><span style="font-weight: 400;">Keep attributes consistent across all variations</span></li><li><span style="font-weight: 400;">Remove unsupported variation labels</span></li></ul>
</div>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-00248fd e-con-full e-flex e-con e-child" data-id="00248fd" data-element_type="container" data-settings="{&quot;_ha_eqh_enable&quot;:false}">

<h2 style="font-size: 24pt; font-weight: 500;">4. Low Quality or Non-Compliant Photos</h2>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-756a6ce elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="756a6ce" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Photos create trust faster than anything else on eBay. They also influence search placement. Poor images cause lower conversions, weaker ranking, and sometimes listing rejection.</span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-4815dec elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="4815dec" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Common issues include low lighting, blurry images, busy backgrounds, watermarks, or stock photos for used products. Since photos help eBay understand your item, this type of eBay listing error reduces the visibility your listing receives.</span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-5f0ed0c elementor-widget elementor-widget-heading" data-id="5f0ed0c" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="heading.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<h3 class="elementor-heading-title elementor-size-default">How to fix it</h3>

<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-4d99f8b elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="4d99f8b" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<ul><li><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Use clear images with even lighting</span></li><li><span style="font-weight: 400;">Capture real product photos from multiple angles</span></li><li><span style="font-weight: 400;">Use a neutral background</span></li><li><span style="font-weight: 400;">Upload at least 500&#215;500 pixels</span></li></ul>
</div>

<h2 style="font-size: 24pt; font-weight: 500;">5. Pricing, Shipping, or Payment Conflicts</h2>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-c5b2059 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="c5b2059" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Even a small mismatch in your settings can block a listing. Sellers often enable features that do not work together, such as immediate payment with ineligible formats or an auction with a Buy It Now price that does not meet requirements.</span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-d2dfac0 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="d2dfac0" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">

<span style="font-weight: 400;">These conflicts confuse the system and generate instant errors.</span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-686c54f elementor-widget elementor-widget-heading" data-id="686c54f" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="heading.default">

<ul><li><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Review all business policies regularly</span></li><li><span style="font-weight: 400;">Keep pricing settings simple and consistent</span></li><li><span style="font-weight: 400;">Confirm that shipping and payment settings match the listing format</span></li></ul>
</div>
