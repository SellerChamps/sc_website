<h2 style="font-size: 24pt; font-weight: 500;">Why Margin Math Gets Messy on Multiple Channels </h2>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-415716a elementor-widget elementor-widget-image" data-id="415716a" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="image.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">

<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-719906a elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="719906a" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Selling on one platform is </span><b>manageable</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">. Selling on five is a </span><b>data challenge</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">; each marketplace has its own fee structure, fulfillment model, and cost layers.</span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-6d36143 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="6d36143" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">According to research, businesses see an average </span><b>revenue increase of 38%</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> when they add a </span><a href="https://supplychain.amazon.com/learn/guide-to-choosing-the-right-sales-channels-for-your-ecommerce-business"><span style="font-weight: 400;">single new sales channel</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">, and up to 190% when they add three.</span>
</div>

<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-3146fb3 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="3146fb3" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">But </span><b>revenue isn&#8217;t profit</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">, and the difference between the two is where most multichannel sellers go wrong…</span>

<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-a6b5be9 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="a6b5be9" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<h3><span style="font-weight: 400;">Here&#8217;s what changes channel by channel:</span></h3>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Referral fees: </b><span style="font-weight: 400;">Platform fees typically range from 8-15% (higher for some categories). This introduces a major variable for profit assessment.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Fulfillment costs: </b><span style="font-weight: 400;">Whether you&#8217;re using Amazon FBA, Walmart Fulfillment Services (WFS), or your own 3PL, per-unit costs differ significantly across platforms.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Monthly subscriptions: </b><span style="font-weight: 400;">Amazon&#8217;s Professional plan runs $39.99/month. Walmart and Etsy have no subscription fees, giving smaller sellers an immediate cost advantage.</span></li>
</ul>

<h2 style="font-size: 24pt; font-weight: 500;">Start with the Core Formula: Gross Profit Margin</h2>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-af7d765 elementor-widget elementor-widget-image" data-id="af7d765" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="image.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<img loading="lazy" loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="1200" height="630" src="https://sellerchamp.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/image4-3.png" class="attachment-full size-full wp-image-3045" alt="" />
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-066e80b elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="066e80b" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Before going channel-specific, you need the baseline formula every seller needs to have memorized.</span>
<b>Gross Profit Margin = (Revenue – COGS) ÷ Revenue × 100</b>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-0b5bb8c elementor-widget elementor-widget-heading" data-id="0b5bb8c" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="heading.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<h3 class="elementor-heading-title elementor-size-default">Track inventory properly</h3>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-97b8a71 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="97b8a71" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Product sourcing or manufacturing </span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Inbound shipping to your warehouse or FBA</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Packaging materials</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Import duties or tariffs</span></li>
</ul>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">For example, if you source a product for $12 and sell it for $30, your gross profit is $18. Your gross margin is 60%. That looks strong, but only until you </span><b>layer in marketplace fees and fulfillment.</b>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-fc58d44 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="fc58d44" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Average gross margins for </span><b>e-commerce businesses</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> typically sit between 40% and 70%. </span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-2a2da17 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="2a2da17" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">But gross margin alone doesn&#8217;t tell you what you&#8217;re actually keeping.</span>

<h2 style="font-size: 24pt; font-weight: 500;">Move to Net Profit Margin: The Number That Actually Matters </h2>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-410cfe0 elementor-widget elementor-widget-image" data-id="410cfe0" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="image.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<img loading="lazy" loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="1200" height="630" src="https://sellerchamp.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/image6-3.png" class="attachment-full size-full wp-image-3047" alt="" />
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-81650f6 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="81650f6" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Net margin is what&#8217;s left </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">after everything</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">. </span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-00a7c92 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="00a7c92" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<p style="text-align: center;"><b>Net Profit Margin = (Net Income ÷ Revenue) × 100</b><a href="https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/accounting/net-profit-margin-formula/#:~:text=Net%20income%20divided%20by%20total%20revenue%2C%20expressed%20as%20a%20percentage"><b>
</b></a></p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-860ad39 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="860ad39" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<a href="https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/resources/accounting/what-is-net-income/"><b>Net income</b></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> = Revenue minus COGS, marketplace fees, fulfillment costs, advertising spend, returns, taxes and overhead.</span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-69bb830 elementor-widget elementor-widget-heading" data-id="69bb830" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="heading.default">

<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Amazon sellers </b><span style="font-weight: 400;">average 5-15% net margin after fees and advertising</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Shopify merchants </b><span style="font-weight: 400;">typically achieve 10-20% net margin with lower platform fees</span></li>

<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<h3 class="elementor-heading-title elementor-size-default">Amazon</h3>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-180a474 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="180a474" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Monthly subscription: </b><span style="font-weight: 400;">$39.99/month (Professional plan) or $0.99/item (Individual plan)</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Referral fees: </b><span style="font-weight: 400;">8-15% per sale; most categories at 15%</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>FBA fees: </b><span style="font-weight: 400;">Variable by weight and size; </span><a href="https://www.novadata.io/resources/blog/amazon-fba-storage-fees"><span style="font-weight: 400;">storage fees spike</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> (jumping over 200%) in Q4 (October-December)</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Advertising: </b><span style="font-weight: 400;">Optional but often necessary; can add 5-15%, or more, to your cost of sale</span></li>
</ul>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Amazon&#8217;s </span><b>seller fee revenue</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> reached</span><b> $140 billion </b><span style="font-weight: 400;">in 2024.</span>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">That&#8217;s money flowing out of seller pockets, which is why precision in </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">calculating your </span><b>per-unit profit margins</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> matters more than ever. </span>

<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-6662aea elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="6662aea" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
