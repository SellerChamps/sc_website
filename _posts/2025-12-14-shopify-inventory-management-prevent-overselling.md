<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<h2 style="font-size: 24pt; font-weight: 500;">What Overselling Really Means</h2>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-719906a elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="719906a" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Overselling is when a customer buys something that you do not actually have available.</span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-6d36143 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="6d36143" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">It usually happens because your inventory is not synced properly. Your product may show “in stock” on Shopify, but it already sold out on another marketplace. Or it might be available in one warehouse, but your store accepts an order from a location that cannot fulfill it.</span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-3146fb3 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="3146fb3" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">The more channels you sell on, the higher the risk becomes. And once it starts happening regularly, it affects your reviews, trust, and future sales.</span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-a6b5be9 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="a6b5be9" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">

<h2 style="font-size: 24pt; font-weight: 500;">Why Shopify Sellers Oversell Across Channels </h2>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-066e80b elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="066e80b" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Most sellers think overselling only happens when they are out of stock. But the real issue is that stock numbers are often delayed or incorrect.</span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-97b8a71 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="97b8a71" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Here are the most common reasons:</span>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Inventory updates are slow across marketplaces</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">You use manual spreadsheets to track stock</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">You have multiple warehouses or locations</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Returns and cancellations are not updated properly</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Bundle products are not tracked correctly</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Two customers buy the last unit at the same time</span></li>
</ul>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">This is where Shopify inventory management becomes tricky, because </span><a href="https://www.google.com/aclk?sa=L&amp;pf=1&amp;ai=DChsSEwifwJO0wtKSAxWlgIMHHc9LARkYACICCAEQABoCZWY&amp;co=1&amp;ase=2&amp;gclid=Cj0KCQiA7rDMBhCjARIsAGDBuEDGMWV2Q7uE_g6xyFZDDLTEw6dk8-qrCgnKbiePf9_67cEx2kGxM9YaAgmoEALw_wcB&amp;cid=CAASuwHkaNPnHM4slxKBm8DFfQ1ix4JU_mHFylSfQT85XCFEacpNe1KSBhDKYZHjdeyk6DsO3L2dZ9DO8GhuQY5FFe54vsk8TcvG4l3z6ReXnIbTbFkYKpb-2VuiBpbdZ5v8Zf6HS3pu1bqPgd-nQGlak1GB5hODHIfZcc4TFYGcFzoVaM5-fmGB8QhFuwsVT-ZmVSIEJHx7tlAULnr_KmlH_KYqNPuXTcFxIDWXGL9Gr0652Gbl_qOkdh46rNtQ&amp;cce=2&amp;category=acrcp_v1_32&amp;sig=AOD64_3piIwwISr0SZSZxs03R_hY3HMlng&amp;q&amp;nis=4&amp;adurl=https://www.shopify.com/ppc/online-store?term%3Dshopify%26adid%3D566143522046%26campaignid%3D15433369425%26branded_enterprise%3D1%26BOID%3Dbrand%26utm_medium%3Dcpc%26utm_source%3Dgoogle%26gad_source%3D1%26gad_campaignid%3D15433369425%26gbraid%3D0AAAAAC3QZJo-9R-fcBIh4oPPs99T3a5Pz%26gclid%3DCj0KCQiA7rDMBhCjARIsAGDBuEDGMWV2Q7uE_g6xyFZDDLTEw6dk8-qrCgnKbiePf9_67cEx2kGxM9YaAgmoEALw_wcB&amp;ved=2ahUKEwjI_Y20wtKSAxU8gv0HHVN6O2kQ0Qx6BAgLEAE"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Shopify </span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">alone is not built to handle complex multichannel inventory without help.</span>

<h2 style="font-size: 24pt; font-weight: 500;">What Shopify Can Do (And What It Cannot) </h2>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-81650f6 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="81650f6" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Shopify has built-in inventory features like:</span>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">stock tracking for products and variants</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">multi-location inventory</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">basic inventory reports</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">manual stock adjustments</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">inventory transfers</span></li>
</ul>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">This is useful for small stores.</span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-00a7c92 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="00a7c92" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">But when you sell across different platforms, you need stronger syncing and </span><a href="https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/automation"><span style="font-weight: 400;">automation</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">. Otherwise, you will keep dealing with mismatched stock and customer complaints.</span>

<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<h2 style="font-size: 24pt; font-weight: 500;">How to Prevent Overselling Across Channels (The Smart Way) </h2>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-d1c34a9 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="d1c34a9" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">To stop overselling, you need a system that updates inventory everywhere, quickly and correctly.</span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-39a2709 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="39a2709" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Here are the most effective steps.</span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-d3b074e elementor-widget elementor-widget-heading" data-id="d3b074e" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="heading.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<h3 class="elementor-heading-title elementor-size-default">Track inventory properly</h3>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-6591d98 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="6591d98" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Make sure inventory tracking is enabled for every product and every variant. Also, disable the setting that allows selling when stock hits zero, unless you intentionally want backorders.</span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-0fba6cd elementor-widget elementor-widget-heading" data-id="0fba6cd" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="heading.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<h3 class="elementor-heading-title elementor-size-default">Keep your inventory synced in real time </h3>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-8af47ea elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="8af47ea" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Make sure inventory tracking is enabled for every product and every variant. Also, disable the setting that allows selling when stock hits zero, unless you intentionally want backorders.</span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-9ff4fb4 elementor-widget elementor-widget-heading" data-id="9ff4fb4" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="heading.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<h3 class="elementor-heading-title elementor-size-default">Use safety stock </h3>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-fc2d157 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="fc2d157" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">A safety stock is a buffer that protects you when syncing delays occur. For example, if you have 20 units, you can set your system to only allow 17 units for sale. This small trick can save you from major problems.</span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-6d9e8cb elementor-widget elementor-widget-heading" data-id="6d9e8cb" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="heading.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<h3 class="elementor-heading-title elementor-size-default">Manage locations carefully </h3>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-7e00e09 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="7e00e09" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">If you store stock in more than one warehouse, Shopify needs to know exactly what is available in each location. If your settings are wrong, Shopify may accept an order that your warehouse cannot fulfill.</span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-fe6a826 elementor-widget elementor-widget-heading" data-id="fe6a826" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="heading.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<h3 class="elementor-heading-title elementor-size-default">Stop relying on spreadsheets </h3>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-f28892c elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="f28892c" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Spreadsheets are fine for planning, but they are not reliable for livestock tracking. Manual updates lead to mistakes, and mistakes lead to overselling.</span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-2caeacd elementor-widget elementor-widget-heading" data-id="2caeacd" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="heading.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<h3 class="elementor-heading-title elementor-size-default">Handle bundles correctly </h3>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-8959d1a elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="8959d1a" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Bundles are a big reason sellers oversell. If you sell a bundle made of multiple items, you need to make sure the stock of each item is updated properly. Otherwise, Shopify will keep selling the bundle even when one part is out of stock.</span>
</div>
</div>
<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-a79f216 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="a79f216" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
<div class="elementor-widget-container">
<span style="font-weight: 400;">These steps make Shopify inventory management smoother and prevent embarrassing stock issues.</span>
</div>
