<h3>2. eBay
</h3>

<p>eBay is perfect for new, used, and collectible items. You can list items for auction or fixed price. Reach buyers worldwide easily.
</p>

<h3>3. Walmart Marketplace
</h3>

<p><a href="https://marketplace.walmart.com/">Walmart Marketplace </a>lets you sell next to a trusted brand. Fees are reasonable, and customers trust Walmart, which helps you close more sales.
</p>

<h3>4. Etsy
</h3>

<p>Etsy is for handmade, vintage, and custom products. Millions of buyers come here for unique items. Perfect for crafters and boutique sellers.
</p>

<h3>5. Temu
</h3>

<p>Temu is fast-growing and attracts bargain hunters. Great for fashion, home goods, and lifestyle products. Quick growth potential for new sellers.
</p>

<h3>6. AliExpress
</h3>

<p>AliExpress connects sellers with buyers worldwide. Affordable products sell well here. Best for sellers wanting international exposure.
</p>

<h3>7. Rakuten
</h3>

<p>Rakuten is strong in Japan and Asia. Loyalty programs help you get repeat buyers. Works for many product types.</p>

</h3>

<p>Poshmark is a peer-to-peer fashion selling platform. Social features help you reach buyers fast. Best for clothing, shoes, and accessories.

<h3>14. Depop
</h3>

<p>Depop is popular with Gen Z. Vintage, streetwear, and unique fashion sell well here. Curated style attracts targeted buyers.</p>

</h3>

<p>For boutique sellers and craft products. Connects small businesses to stores. Good for bulk and recurring orders.</p>
