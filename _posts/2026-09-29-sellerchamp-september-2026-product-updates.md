September was one of the biggest months we have shipped in a while. Two brand-new sales channels went live, the catalog got a major batch import workflow, and suppliers gained new controls over which products reach each channel. Here is everything that landed this month.

<h2>Spotlight Feature
</h2>

<h3>TikTok Shop and Back Market Are Now Live in SellerChamp
</h3>

<p>Two high-growth channels, TikTok Shop and Back Market, are now fully connected inside SellerChamp.</p>

<h3>What's New:
</h3>

<p><b>TikTok Shop</b> is now a supported selling channel inside SellerChamp. You can list, sync inventory, and manage orders from TikTok Shop alongside every other channel you already sell on. Key capabilities include:</p>

<ul>
<li>List products to TikTok Shop directly from your SellerChamp catalog</li>
<li>Real-time inventory sync across TikTok Shop and all connected channels</li>
<li>TikTok Shop orders flow into the unified orders queue</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Back Market</b> support gives sellers a direct path into the leading refurbished goods marketplace:</p>

<ul>
<li>List refurbished and pre-owned products to Back Market from SellerChamp</li>
<li>Back Market orders sync automatically into your orders queue</li>
<li>Inventory levels stay accurate across all channels in real time</li>
</ul>

<h3>Why It Matters:
</h3>

<p>TikTok Shop is one of the fastest-growing commerce platforms today. Back Market dominates the refurbished device space in both North America and Europe. Adding both channels through SellerChamp means no new tools, no separate logins, and no duplicate work. Your catalog and inventory rules apply the same way they do everywhere else.</p>

<h2>Catalog Updates
</h2>

<h3>Catalog Batch Imports
</h3>

<p>You can now build and update your catalog in bulk through a structured batch import workflow.</p>

<h3>What's New:
</h3>

<ul>
<li>Import large product catalogs using CSV templates</li>
<li>Create new catalog products or update existing ones in a single upload</li>
<li>Map fields during import so your data lands in the right place</li>
<li>Failed rows come back with error details so nothing is silently dropped</li>
<li>Imports run in the background so large files do not block your workflow</li>
</ul>

<h3>Why It Matters:
</h3>

<p>Building a catalog product by product is not practical at scale. Batch imports let you bring in hundreds or thousands of products at once, whether you are onboarding a new supplier feed, cleaning up existing data, or expanding into new categories.</p>

<h2>Supplier Controls
</h2>

<h3>Control Which Items Each Supplier Sends
</h3>

<p>Supplier item controls give you precise control over which products a supplier can and cannot push into your catalog.</p>

<h3>What's New:
</h3>

<ul>
<li>Set include and exclude lists at the supplier level</li>
<li>Control which supplier items are eligible to update catalog products</li>
<li>Rules apply automatically on every feed run. Set them once and they hold</li>
</ul>

<h3>Why It Matters:
</h3>

<p>Suppliers often send more products than you want to carry or update. These controls let you decide exactly which supplier items affect your catalog without manually filtering every feed.</p>

<h2>Channel Exclusion Lists
</h2>

<h3>Per-Channel Include and Exclude Lists
</h3>

<p>Amazon, eBay, Etsy, BigCommerce, Google Merchant, Macy's, and data-feed accounts can now each keep their own include and exclude lists.</p>

<h3>What's New:
</h3>

<ul>
<li>Each channel account has its own include list and exclude list</li>
<li>Use them to enforce brand or manufacturer exclusions per channel</li>
<li>Toggle MAP (Minimum Advertised Price) enforcement on or off per channel</li>
<li>Rules set at the channel level apply automatically to every feed linked to that account. Define it once and it holds everywhere, without setting it per feed</li>
</ul>

<h3>Why It Matters:
</h3>

<p>Different channels have different rules. Some brands restrict where their products can be listed. Some channels require MAP enforcement. Per-channel lists let you manage these requirements cleanly without duplicating effort across every feed.</p>

<h2>Fulfillment Updates
</h2>

<h3>Route Order Items to Different Suppliers
</h3>

<p>A single order can now be split across multiple suppliers at the line-item level.</p>

<h3>What's New:
</h3>

<ul>
<li>Each line item in an order can be routed to a different supplier independently</li>
<li>If an order has two items, item one can go to Supplier A and item two to Supplier B</li>
<li>No manual splitting or workarounds required. Routing happens at the item level</li>
</ul>

<h3>Why It Matters:
</h3>

<p>Multi-supplier fulfillment is common for sellers with broad catalogs. When an order contains items from different suppliers, routing each item to the right source automatically removes a manual step and reduces the chance of fulfillment errors.</p>

Every update this month is designed to give you more control with less manual work. Thanks for your continued feedback and support. <b>Team SellerChamp</b></p>
