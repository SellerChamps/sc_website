October focused on sharper pricing controls, a cleaner catalog workflow, and a long list of improvements across nearly every part of the platform. The headline is a new repricing strategy that targets the median price instead of the floor, available now on both Amazon and Walmart. Here is everything that shipped this month.

<h2>Spotlight Feature
</h2>

<h3>Compete Against the Median Price on Amazon and Walmart
</h3>

<p>Both the Amazon and Walmart repricers now support a Median Price strategy under Compete For.</p>

<h3>What's New:
</h3>

<p>The Buy Box and lowest price are not always the right target. Sometimes the smart move is the middle, and now you can go there.</p>

<p><b>Amazon:</b></p>

<ul>
<li>Go to Settings → Selling Channels → Amazon → Pricing Rules</li>
<li>Set Compete For to Median Price</li>
<li>Your repricer targets the median offer instead of the Buy Box or the floor</li>
</ul>

<p><img src="https://cdn.mcauto-images-production.sendgrid.net/45174282d0a602d8/0c0e6193-4f44-409e-b402-26b35def4588/1976x1024.png" alt="" loading="lazy"></p>

<p><b>Walmart:</b></p>

<ul>
<li>The same option is now in Settings → Selling Channels → Walmart → Pricing Rules</li>
<li>One consistent repricing strategy, available across both channels</li>
</ul>

<p><img src="https://cdn.mcauto-images-production.sendgrid.net/45174282d0a602d8/16e07375-c224-456e-aa1c-ff86a7990453/1970x1010.png" alt="" loading="lazy"></p>

<h3>Why It Matters:
</h3>

<p>Racing to the floor protects the Buy Box but destroys margin. Targeting the median lets you stay competitive while holding a reasonable price. For sellers with strong listings, the median is often a better position than the cheapest offer on the page.</p>

<h2>Catalog Updates
</h2>

<h3>Auto-Assign Catalogue SKUs
</h3>

<p>New catalog products can now get the next sequential SKU automatically.</p>

<h3>What's New:
</h3>

<ul>
<li>Go to Settings → Account Setup → New Catalog Product SKU</li>
<li>Switch from "Enter SKU, or use UPC/ASIN from scan" to "Auto-assign next sequential number"</li>
<li>Any product added without a SKU gets the next number in sequence automatically</li>
</ul>

<p><img src="https://cdn.mcauto-images-production.sendgrid.net/45174282d0a602d8/65ef13c5-b14d-4850-802b-ff6d3a912ced/2206x1120.png" alt="" loading="lazy"></p>

<h3>Why It Matters:
</h3>

<p>Manually assigning SKUs to every new product is a small task that adds up fast. Auto-assignment keeps your SKU format consistent and removes one more thing to remember when adding products.</p>

<h3>Delete a Catalogue Product
</h3>

<p>You can now delete a catalog product directly from the catalog list.</p>

<h3>What's New:
</h3>

<ul>
<li>Find the row menu (bars icon) on any catalog product and select Delete</li>
<li>Choose what happens to its marketplace listings: end them, delete them, or leave them as they are</li>
<li>Deletion is confirmed before it runs</li>
</ul>

<p><img src="https://cdn.mcauto-images-production.sendgrid.net/45174282d0a602d8/9d40583d-6e84-4e59-ba77-6daadb176b5d/2190x1088.png" alt="" loading="lazy"></p>

<h3>Why It Matters:
</h3>

<p>Previously, removing a product from the catalog required workarounds. Direct deletion with listing handling options gives you a clean, controlled way to retire products you no longer carry.</p>

<h2>Shopify Updates
</h2>

<h3>Import Only Shopify Products with a Required Metafield
</h3>

<p>You can now require a metafield to be present before a Shopify product is imported into SellerChamp.</p>

<h3>What's New:
</h3>

<ul>
<li>Set a required metafield name at Settings → Account Setup</li>
<li>Override the setting per Shopify store in Settings → Selling Channels → Shopify → Account Information</li>
<li>Only products that have the specified metafield are imported</li>
<li>Products already imported continue syncing normally</li>
<li>Leave the field blank to import all products as before</li>
</ul>

<p><img src="https://cdn.mcauto-images-production.sendgrid.net/45174282d0a602d8/10ed3a20-6e8a-4aac-9af2-8e1dd84eff4a/1986x1012.png" alt="" loading="lazy"></p>

<h3>Why It Matters:
</h3>

<p>Large Shopify stores often contain products that are not ready for marketplace listing. A required metafield filter lets your team tag products as listing-ready before SellerChamp picks them up, keeping your catalog clean without manual filtering.</p>

<h2>Improvements and Enhancements
</h2>

<h3>Catalog Enhancements
</h3>

<ul>
<li><b>Average Cost Price on the Catalogue Product</b>: The catalog product edit window now has an Avg Cost Price field so you can track average cost alongside all your other product data</li>
</ul>

<p><img src="https://cdn.mcauto-images-production.sendgrid.net/45174282d0a602d8/f262c921-724b-4e1e-9d7f-cad3b8ff76a6/2176x1080.png" alt="" loading="lazy"></p>

<ul>
<li><b>Received Items That Still Need a Listing</b>: On the receive step of a catalog batch, you can now see how many received items still need a listing, with a direct link back to Add Items so nothing slips through</li>
</ul>

<p><img src="https://cdn.mcauto-images-production.sendgrid.net/45174282d0a602d8/dd9adce7-8b29-4618-9568-61a919246f52/2216x1130.png" alt="" loading="lazy"></p>

<ul>
<li><b>MSRP in Revision History</b>: MSRP changes now appear in revision history so you can see exactly when a price was set or cleared on any product</li>
</ul>

<p><img src="https://cdn.mcauto-images-production.sendgrid.net/45174282d0a602d8/990a931f-0083-4586-a141-a55865b3b35f/2332x1148.png" alt="" loading="lazy"></p>

<ul>
<li><b>Listings Linked Back After Duplicate SKU</b>: Channel listings that ended up attached to the wrong duplicate catalog row are now linked back to the correct matching product automatically</li>
<li><b>Bundle Quantity Follows the Catalogue Part</b>: When a catalog sync updates a product that is part of a bundle, the bundle's quantity now refreshes to reflect the change</li>
</ul>

<h3>Amazon Enhancements
</h3>

<ul>
<li><b>Business Price Stays at or Above Your Minimum</b>: An Amazon business price that would have been sent below your minimum is now raised to that minimum automatically, so you never accidentally undercut yourself on business pricing</li>
</ul>

<h3>Walmart Enhancements
</h3>

<ul>
<li><b>Walmart Logo on Shipping Rates</b>: Walmart Shipping rates in the label window now show the Walmart logo, making it easier to tell them apart from other carriers at a glance</li>
</ul>

<p><img src="https://cdn.mcauto-images-production.sendgrid.net/45174282d0a602d8/3f1c251d-5ce3-4fd2-bc63-c70d28bcc785/1920x1011.png" alt="" loading="lazy"></p>

<ul>
<li><b>Account Stays Active After a Rejected Ship-Node Request</b>: A Walmart account is no longer marked inactive just because one order ship-node request was rejected</li>
<li><b>Copied Description Kept on a UPC Match</b>: When a Walmart listing is copied and matches the catalog by UPC, the description you copied is preserved instead of being overwritten</li>
</ul>

<h3>eBay and Etsy Enhancements
</h3>

<ul>
<li><b>Clearer eBay Listing Error Details</b>: When eBay rejects a listing, the error detail now includes the extra parameters eBay sent, not just the short message, so you can diagnose issues faster</li>
</ul>

<p><img src="https://cdn.mcauto-images-production.sendgrid.net/45174282d0a602d8/0dcc9a62-d888-4705-8a7c-4cb86515273e/1962x920.png" alt="" loading="lazy"></p>

<ul>
<li><b>Updated Etsy "When It Was Made" Years</b>: The Etsy year ranges now match Etsy's 2026 list, including 2020-2026 and Before 2007</li>
</ul>

<p><img src="https://cdn.mcauto-images-production.sendgrid.net/45174282d0a602d8/82fb8aa3-61b7-422f-908d-59cbfc545eb9/1956x1012.png" alt="" loading="lazy"></p>

<h3>Repricing and Reporting Enhancements
</h3>

<ul>
<li><b>Easier-to-Read Repricing Events</b>: Opening a repricing event now shows the offer data in a clean table, highlights your own offer as "Your Offer", and lets you toggle the raw event text on or off</li>
<li><b>Repricer Floor Restored After Supplier Price</b>: If a feed tries another supplier's price and that price is not used, the listing's starting price is put back correctly</li>
<li><b>Sales Report Can Include SKUs with No Sales</b>: The SKU sales export can now include SKUs that sold nothing in the period. Find it under Insights → SKUs → Include SKUs with no sales</li>
</ul>

<p><img src="https://cdn.mcauto-images-production.sendgrid.net/45174282d0a602d8/2bcfabe7-2696-4d9b-bd36-85eb4509318b/1992x1010.png" alt="" loading="lazy"></p>

<ul>
<li><b>Repricer Usage on the Subscription Page</b>: Settings → Billing and Subscription now shows how many repricers you are using against your plan limit</li>
</ul>

<h2>Final Thoughts
</h2>

<p>October brought a smarter repricing strategy, a cleaner catalog workflow, and improvements that touch almost every channel in the platform. Every update is designed to give you more control and less manual work. Thanks for your continued feedback. <b>Team SellerChamp</b></p>
