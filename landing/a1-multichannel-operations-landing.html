Common questions

Answered before your demo call

How long does it take to get set up?+

Most sellers are live within 3–5 business days. Our onboarding team handles the channel connections and initial catalog import with you.

We have 50,000+ SKUs. Can SellerChamp handle that?+

Yes. SellerChamp is built for large catalogs. Bulk import via CSV, supplier feed (API/FTP), or barcode scan. Customers manage hundreds of thousands of SKUs on the platform.

We’re currently on Linnworks / ChannelAdvisor. How hard is it to migrate?+

We’ve migrated dozens of sellers from those platforms. Our team handles the data migration and runs both systems in parallel until you’re confident. Most migrations complete in under 2 weeks.

Will I lose my listings or inventory data when I migrate?+

No. We migrate your existing listings, inventory quantities, pricing rules, and templates. Your live listings on Amazon, eBay, Walmart, and Shopify stay active throughout the migration — there’s no downtime and no gap in selling. We run a parallel sync until you’re confident everything is correct before cutting over.

Do you require an annual contract or cancellation fee?+

None. SellerChamp is month-to-month — cancel any time with no penalty. Compare that to SellerCloud and Rithum which lock you into 12-month contracts with hefty exit clauses. We earn your business every single month.

Custom quote based on SKU count, monthly order volume, channels connected, and integrations required. Most enterprise customers land between $399 and $2,500/month. No annual contract required — month-to-month. We send the quote within 24 hours of the demo.
