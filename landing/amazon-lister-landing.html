FAQs

Common questions from Amazon sellers.

How does the Bulk Lister work?
Scan barcodes or upload a bulk file. SellerChamp pulls in product data so you aren’t typing titles, photos, and item specifics by hand, then publishes the listings to Amazon and your other channels.
How do I create new ASINs?
The ASIN creator fills in required fields, maps the category, and lines the listing up with Amazon’s attribute requirements — which cuts down on rejections when you’re creating products that don’t already have an ASIN.
Can I customize listings for different channels?
Yes. Tailor titles, categories, and item specifics for each marketplace so the same product meets Amazon’s requirements and the requirements of eBay, Walmart, Shopify, and the rest.
How long does it take to list products with SellerChamp?
With the Bulk Lister you can list thousands of products across multiple channels in minutes, instead of building each Amazon listing one at a time.
Does this work with Amazon FBA?
Yes. SellerChamp works with FBA, seller-fulfilled (FBM), and hybrid setups. FBA inventory stays in sync, and you can use Multi-Channel Fulfillment to ship orders that come in on other marketplaces.
Can I list my Amazon products on other marketplaces?
Yes. Cross-list Amazon inventory to eBay, Walmart, Shopify, Etsy, and other connected platforms, and pull listings the other direction when you want Amazon to be the new channel.
How does the inventory sync work?
When a unit sells on Amazon, available quantity updates on your other connected stores so you don’t oversell. The same sync runs when a sale happens on eBay, Walmart, or another channel.
Is my Amazon seller account safe?
Yes. SellerChamp connects through Amazon’s official Selling Partner API with OAuth. We never ask you to store your Amazon password in SellerChamp.
How long does setup take?
Most sellers are up and running in under 30 minutes: connect Amazon, import products, and start syncing. Setup support is included if you want a walkthrough.
