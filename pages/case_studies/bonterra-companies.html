“Before SellerChamp, managing our listings and inventory manually was a nightmare. It was tedious, error-prone, and held back our ability to scale.”

Martin Evenson, CEO, Bonterra Companies

Bonterra Companies is a Texas-based reseller specializing in air conditioning units. With a small team and an innovative approach that includes bundles and kits, they needed more than a rigid off-the-shelf tool — they needed automation that could bend to their model.

Challenges before SellerChamp

  • Manual Excel workflows for inventory and listings — slow, error-prone, and hard to keep current.
  • Limited flexibility in existing tools (Shopify, ShipStation, WooCommerce) for custom bundles, kits, and pricing.
  • Scaling blocked — expanding to new channels while managing existing ones was nearly impossible without automation.

How SellerChamp helped

  • Bundles & kits — link products with accurate pricing without spreadsheet gymnastics.
  • Real-time inventory — automatic sync across channels to prevent overselling.
  • Multichannel listing — publish and manage listings from one system as they expand.
  • Custom workflows & reports — tailored to how Bonterra actually sells, not a one-size template.

Results

  • Hours of manual listing and inventory work replaced with automation
  • Confidence to expand to additional sales channels
  • Fewer stock errors and cleaner operations around bundles and kits

“If SellerChamp were gone, it would give me a 100% heart attack. It would collapse my business entirely.”

Martin Evenson
