Fragrance Spot is a family-owned health and beauty reseller in Florida. When foot traffic dropped during COVID-19, they moved online — and quickly hit the limits of manual inventory sync, order processing, and price updates across Shopify and ShipStation.

Challenges before SellerChamp

  • Manual inventory syncing with suppliers — stockouts, overselling, and missed sales.
  • Slow fulfillment — orders processed one-by-one with frequent errors.
  • Little automation — hard to scale without adding headcount.
  • Labor-intensive pricing — no automated way to stay competitive.

How SellerChamp helped

  • FTP / supplier sync with Shopify — inventory stays accurate without daily spreadsheet updates.
  • Pick & pack automation — automated pick lists for faster, cleaner fulfillment.
  • Dynamic repricing — adjust prices from competition and velocity without constant manual work.
  • Centralized orders — manage marketplace and cart orders in one place.
  • Custom workflows & reports — visibility into inventory, sales, and fulfillment.

Why Fragrance Spot chose SellerChamp

They evaluated Sellercloud, Skubana, and ChannelAdvisor. SellerChamp won on affordability, Shopify/FTP inventory sync, customizable workflows, and hands-on support during implementation.

Results

  • Supplier inventory sync that saves hours of manual work each week
  • Faster, more accurate order fulfillment
  • Competitive pricing without constant manual updates
  • Operations that rely on SellerChamp for nearly 100% of day-to-day work

“SellerChamp is the backbone of our operations. It connects everything and handles it all. We wouldn’t be able to operate at this scale without it.”

Fragrance Spot Owner
