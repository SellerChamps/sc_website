Who each fits

Enterprise commerce network vs marketplace ops Platform

Rithum is the brand name for ChannelAdvisor today. Both names still appear in contracts and RFPs. This page compares marketplace operations fit, not every retail media or advertising module Rithum sells.

Rithum (ChannelAdvisor)

  • Enterprise brands, retailers, and large catalog teams
  • Channel programs with services and implementation projects
  • Custom contracts; annual terms common
  • Retail media and marketplace advertising depth

SellerChamp Platform

  • High-SKU sellers on Amazon, eBay, Walmart, Shopify, and resale channels
  • Bulk listing, sync, orders, routing, repricing, and feeds in one login
  • Month-to-month quotes; no GMV fees on standard Platform quotes
  • Free migration; live in days, not a quarter-long project

FAQ

SellerChamp vs Rithum questions

Is Rithum the same as ChannelAdvisor?+

Yes. Rithum is the current brand for the company many sellers still call ChannelAdvisor. Product packaging and contract names may still say ChannelAdvisor.

When should I switch from Rithum to SellerChamp?+

When subscription and services cost, contract lock-in, or slow change cycles hurt more than account features help. Common triggers are margin pressure from fees, listing backlog, or needing bulk intake and fulfillment rules without opening another ticket.

Does SellerChamp replace retail media tools?+

No. SellerChamp is built for listing, inventory, orders, and fulfillment. If retail media is the main reason you keep Rithum, map those modules separately before you move ops.

How long does migration take?+

Many Platform sellers go live in 2 to 5 business days after onboarding starts. Large catalogs may run parallel sync longer. Your specialist scopes timing on the demo.
