FAQ

SellerChamp vs Sellbrite questions

When should I switch from Sellbrite to SellerChamp?+

When catalog size, channel count, or fulfillment rules outgrow a list-sync-ship stack. Common triggers are oversell risk, manual reconciliation, liquidation manifests, or needing repricing and routing without a second tool.

Does SellerChamp cost more than Sellbrite?+

Platform pricing is higher than entry Sellbrite tiers because it includes more modules and human onboarding. Total cost often beats enterprise suites that charge GMV or annual lock-in.

Can SellerChamp import my Sellbrite catalog?+

Yes. Migration is included on Platform quotes. Listings and inventory can run in parallel until you cut over.
