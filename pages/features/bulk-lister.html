Photo to listing

Catalog mapping

Map your catalog to each channel correctly

Maintain one product catalog, then map categories, item specifics, and variations to what each marketplace expects — so listings go live with fewer errors and less per-channel rework.

Category mapping

Map your internal categories to Amazon browse nodes, eBay categories, Walmart taxonomies, and other channel trees so products land in the right place every time you publish.

Item specifics & attributes

Translate your catalog attributes into channel-required item specifics — brand, size, material, condition, and more — so listings meet marketplace validation rules instead of bouncing back incomplete.

Variation mapping

Push parent/child variations (size, color, style, and other options) with the structure each channel expects, so multi-SKU families stay consistent across marketplaces.

FAQ

Bulk Lister questions

How does the Bulk Lister work?+

Scan barcodes or upload bulk files. SellerChamp auto-fetches product data and helps you publish listings across connected channels faster.

How do I create new ASINs?+

SellerChamp helps auto-populate required fields, map categories correctly, and reduce listing rejections when creating Amazon ASINs.

Can I customize listings for different channels?+

Yes. Tailor product data for each selling channel so listings meet marketplace-specific requirements.

Does this work with Amazon FBA?+

Yes. SellerChamp works with Amazon FBA, FBM, and hybrid models, and can sync FBA inventory automatically.

Can I list Amazon products on other marketplaces?+

Yes. Cross-list Amazon inventory to eBay, Walmart, Shopify, Etsy, and additional platforms from one catalog.
