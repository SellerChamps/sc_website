Inventory sync questions

What is inventory sync, and how does it work?+

Inventory sync automatically updates stock quantities across sales channels. With SellerChamp, inventory stays accurate across platforms like eBay, Amazon, Etsy, and Walmart so you avoid overselling.

Which sales channels are supported?+

SellerChamp supports Amazon, eBay, Etsy, Walmart, Shopify, and many more — with inventory managed from one place.

How does SellerChamp prevent overselling?+

When a sale happens, SellerChamp updates stock quantities across connected channels so available inventory stays consistent.

Can SellerChamp handle variations and bundles?+

Yes. Variations, bundles, and kits are supported and kept in sync across your sales channels.

Is there a limit to how many products can be synced?+

SellerChamp is built for large catalogs — from a few hundred SKUs to hundreds of thousands.
