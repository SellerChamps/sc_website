FAQ

RePricer questions

Does SellerChamp include a repricer?+

Yes. RePricer is built into SellerChamp for Amazon, Walmart, and other connected channels. You set margin floors and rules; prices update automatically without a separate repricing subscription.

How does SellerChamp’s Repricer help win the Buy Box?+

Rule-based algorithms match or beat competitors while respecting your profit goals, adjusting prices in real time across major selling platforms.

Can I set different repricing rules for different products?+

Yes. Create multiple rules by category, brand, or inventory level so each SKU follows the strategy you want.

What is Price Protection?+

Price Protection watches selling-price changes. If a price moves by more than your configured percent threshold, the product is flagged for review so large swings don’t go unnoticed.

Does it work across Amazon, Walmart, eBay, and Etsy?+

Yes. SellerChamp’s multi-channel repricing keeps strategy consistent across Amazon, Walmart, eBay, Etsy, Shopify, and other connected channels.

How is this different from traditional repricers?+

Traditional tools often rely on slow, fixed undercutting. SellerChamp’s dynamic RePricer uses richer signals, competition, inventory, fulfillment, and more, to find competitive prices without racing to zero.
