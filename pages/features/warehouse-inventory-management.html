Why it matters

Your warehouse is the source of truth, not the marketplace

Spreadsheets and manual counts fall apart once you are listing the same SKUs across five channels. Warehouse inventory management software tracks exactly what you have, where it sits, and what needs reordering, then feeds that truth to every marketplace you sell on.

Know what you have, by location

Barcode scans replace manual counts, so on-hand quantity and BIN location stay accurate as stock moves through receiving, putaway, and picking.

Run more than one warehouse

Track owned warehouses, FBA, and 3PL stock side by side, with routing rules that send each order to the right location.

Reorder before you stock out

Forecasting on sales velocity flags SKUs approaching their reorder point, so purchasing happens ahead of a stockout, not after lost sales.

FAQ

Warehouse inventory management questions

What is warehouse inventory management software?+

Software that tracks stock by physical location inside one or more warehouses, using barcode scans to record receiving, putaway, picking, and shipping, so the system always knows what is on hand and where.

How is this different from inventory sync?+

Inventory sync keeps marketplace quantities aligned after a sale. Warehouse inventory management governs what happens before that: receiving, scanning, BIN assignment, and tracking inside the warehouse. See the inventory sync page for the channel side.
