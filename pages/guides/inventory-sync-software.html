Without sync, channel A and channel B can both show full stock after a sale on only one channel. - q: Does inventory sync work with Amazon FBA? a: Yes. Basics

Why sellers adopt inventory sync

Protect account health

Oversells lead to cancellations, defects, and lost Buy Box. Sync reduces those hits as catalog size grows.

Scale to more channels

You can list the same SKU in more places when quantity stays truthful in one system.

Feed warehouse truth

Connect FBA, 3PL, and in-house locations so ops teams stop reconciling spreadsheets.
