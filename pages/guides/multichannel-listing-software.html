How it works

What good listing software should do

One catalog, many channels

Maintain a single SKU record, then map browse nodes, item specifics, and variation rules per marketplace.

Publish listings in bulk

Publish at scale from CSV import, barcode scan, UPC/ASIN lookup, supplier feeds (API/FTP), or liquidation manifests. You skip typing each listing by hand.

Listing + inventory together

Publishing is only half the job. Pair listing with real-time inventory sync so new channels do not create oversell risk.

SellerChamp

Where SellerChamp fits

SellerChamp is built for operators with large catalogs and five or more channels. Bulk Lister covers publish workflows; Platform adds sync, orders, fulfillment, repricing, and feeds.
