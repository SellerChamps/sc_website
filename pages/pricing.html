Pricing questions, answered

How does pricing work?+

Custom quote based on SKU count, monthly order volume, channels connected, and integrations required. Most customers land between $399 and $999/month; larger enterprise setups can go higher. Month-to-month — no annual contract required.

Are there GMV or per-channel fees?+

No GMV percentage and no surprise per-channel nickel-and-diming. Your quote covers the scope we agree on in the demo.

Do you require an annual contract?+

No. SellerChamp is month-to-month. Cancel any time with no penalty. Prefer to pay annually? Annual subscriptions get a 20% discount.
