# Pricing — SellerChamp

Last updated: 2026-11-17

SellerChamp uses custom quotes for the full Platform based on catalog size, monthly order volume, connected channels, and required modules. Most Platform customers land between **$399 and $999 USD per month**. Quotes are sent within about 24 hours after a demo.

## Platform (custom quote)

- **Price:** $399+/month starting; **$399–$999/month typical** for high-SKU multichannel ops; Enterprise custom above that
- **Billing:** Month-to-month by default; **20% discount** if paid annually
- **GMV fees:** None
- **Per-channel fees:** None advertised on standard quotes
- **Annual contract:** Not required
- **Onboarding:** Included for Platform; migration support included

### Growth (reference tier on marketing site)

- **From:** $399/month
- **Includes:** Multichannel listing, inventory sync, core order management, standard channel mix, email and chat support

### Scale (reference tier on marketing site)

- **Typical:** $399–$999/month
- **Includes:** Growth features plus repricer, forecasting, vendor feeds, multi-warehouse / FBA workflows, priority onboarding

### Enterprise (reference tier on marketing site)

- **Price:** Custom
- **Includes:** Very large SKU counts, advanced API/ERP integrations, custom workflows, SLAs, named onboarding owner

## Self-serve lister plans

Lister-focused signups (eBay/Amazon lister campaigns) may use separate Starter / Growth / Pro tiers advertised on paid landing pages (e.g. from $29/month). Confirm current tiers on the live signup flow before quoting to buyers.

- **Trial:** 14-day trials on lister landings where advertised; no credit card on some flows
- **Signup:** https://app.sellerchamp.com/users/sign_up
